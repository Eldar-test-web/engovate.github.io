FAQs

Are these recurring payments?

No. All Engovate plans are one-time payments. You pay once for the duration of your choice (1, 3, 6, or 12 months) and you will never be charged automatically again.

Can I get a refund if I’m not satisfied?

Because we provide extensive free tools and trials to test the service, all Premium payments are non-refundable. We encourage you to try the Free plan thoroughly before upgrading.

Can I log in on multiple devices?

Yes! You can be logged into your Engovate account on up to two devices simultaneously (for example, your laptop and your smartphone). If you log into a third device, your session on the first device will be automatically logged out.

Can I share my account with a friend?

Yes, you can! We don’t mind if you share your account with a friend to study together. However, please be aware that sharing your login credentials may cause you to be constantly logged out if you or your friend accidentally log in on a third device. Additionally, all progress tracking and AI evaluation history will be shared within the same account profile.

Does Engovate support mobile devices?

Yes, our website is mobile-responsive. However, for the best experience – especially when using the AI Writing and Speaking tools – we recommend using Chromium-based browsers.

Can I upgrade from a 1-month plan to a 12-month plan?

Yes! You can purchase a longer-duration plan at any time. If you have active days remaining on your current plan, please contact our support team to help you merge your access.

I paid for Premium but my account hasn’t updated. What should I do?

After purchasing, you should receive a premium code in your email. Please follow this link on How to Activate a Premium Code.

What is the difference between “Free” and “Premium” plans?

The Free plan is designed for casual learners who aren’t in a rush. The Premium plan is built for serious candidates who want to boost their band score quickly through unlimited practice and progress tracking.

Can I buy a subscription code now and activate it later? Will the subscription code expire?

Your subscription code will not expire. You can save it and activate it whenever you are ready. Your subscription time does not start when you purchase or receive the code – it only begins counting down from the exact moment you activate it on your account.

Does the Premium plan guarantee I will pass the IELTS?

While we cannot guarantee a score, Engovate strives to offer every tool needed to help you achieve your goal. From unlimited AI-powered evaluations and real-time speaking practice to personalized progress tracking, our platform is designed to give you the highest quality resources possible. When you combine these tools with your own hard work and consistent practice, you give yourself the best possible chance of success.

How accurate is the AI Writing and Speaking evaluation?

Our AI-powered checkers are designed to provide instant, detailed feedback based on official IELTS criteria. Our evaluations typically have an accuracy rate of 75% to 95% compared to official scores. It is exceptionally good at catching grammatical errors, identifying repetitive vocabulary, and checking if your essay structure meets the task requirements. We recommend using it as a highly reliable guide to identify your strengths and weaknesses during your preparation, but not as a substitute for professional assessment or official IELTS grading.

Why did I get a different score than my teacher/another tool?

IELTS grading can sometimes be subjective. Our AI is often slightly stricter than human teachers to ensure you are fully prepared for the real exam. If our AI gives you a 6.5, it’s a sign that you should push a little harder to ensure a solid 7.0 on test day. Remember to focus on the detailed feedback – the specific suggestions for grammar and vocabulary are often more valuable than the band score itself!

How close are the practice tests to the real IELTS exam?

Our materials are as close as you can get to the actual exam experience. All of our practice tests are sourced directly from official Cambridge IELTS books.

I’m having trouble with the Speaking recorder. What should I do?

If you’re having trouble with the Speaking recorder, it is most likely due to your browser permissions or an unstable Wi-Fi connection. First, please ensure you have granted microphone access by clicking “Allow” in your browser’s popup (you can check this by clicking the lock icon in your address bar). Additionally, because our AI requires a steady connection to upload and analyze your voice, a weak or fluctuating Wi-Fi signal can cause the recorder to fail; try switching to a more stable connection or using mobile data if the issue persists.

How can I contact support if I have a problem?

We are here to help! You can reach out to our support team via the Contact Us page or email us directly at helloengovate@gmail.com. We typically respond within 24 hours.
