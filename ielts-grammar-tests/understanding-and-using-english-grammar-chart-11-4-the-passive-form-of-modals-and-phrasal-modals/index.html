Understanding and Using English Grammar Chart 11-4 – The Passive Form Of Modals and Phrasal Modals

Choose or type the answer to each question, then press Check to see if you are right.
  1. Does "Applicants must be able to speak two languages fluently" contain a passive modal?
    Must be able to speak is an active phrasal modal: able is an adjective, not a past participle. The applicants do the speaking themselves.
  2. Does "Delivery drivers must be calm and patient in heavy traffic" contain a passive modal?
    Here must be is followed by adjectives (calm, patient), not a past participle, so there is no passive modal.
  3. Does "All new employees must complete an online safety course" contain a passive modal?
    Must complete is an active modal (modal + simple form). The employees do the action, and there is no be + past participle.
  4. Does "Team leaders will be expected to train new staff members" contain a passive modal?
    Will be expected is modal + be + past participle, so it is a passive modal: the company expects this of the team leaders.
  5. Does "Volunteers may be sent to other cities after a disaster" contain a passive modal?
    May be sent is modal + be + past participle. Someone sends the volunteers, so it is a passive modal.
  6. Does "Passengers' bags may be searched at the border" contain a passive modal?
    May be searched is a passive modal: officers search the bags, and the bags receive the action.
  7. Does "Cell phones may not be used during the exam" contain a passive modal?
    May not be used is a negative passive modal: modal + not + be + past participle.
  8. Does "Customer complaints must be handled politely" contain a passive modal?
    Must be handled is modal + be + past participle, so it is a passive modal: the staff handle the complaints.
  9. Does "Patient records must be carefully protected" contain a passive modal?
    Must be protected is a passive modal. An adverb such as carefully can come between be and the past participle.
  10. Does "Interns will be required to give a short presentation each month" contain a passive modal?
    Will be required is modal + be + past participle, so it is a passive modal.
  11. Does "Applicants have to really enjoy working with children" contain a passive modal?
    Have to enjoy is an active phrasal modal + the simple form of a verb. There is no be + past participle.
  12. Passengers ___ (should + tell) about the delay before they board the plane.
    Answer: should be told
    Passengers receive the information, so the verb is passive: should + be + past participle (told).
  13. The airline ___ (should + tell) passengers about delays as early as possible.
    Answer: should tell
    The airline does the telling and passengers is the object, so the verb is active: should + simple form.
  14. The staff ___ (should + tell) about the office move weeks ago, not the day before.
    Answer: should have been told
    Weeks ago shows a past situation that should have happened but didn't, so we use should + have been + past participle.
  15. In this lab, all chemicals ___ (must + keep) in a locked cabinet overnight.
    Answer: must be kept
    Someone keeps the chemicals in the cabinet; the chemicals receive the action, so the passive modal is must + be + kept.
  16. Campers ___ (must + keep) all their food in sealed boxes because of the bears.
    Answer: must keep
    The campers do the action and food is the object, so the active form must keep is correct.
  17. This old suitcase ___ (can't + open) because nobody remembers the code for the lock.
    Accepted answers: can't be opened / cannot be opened
    The suitcase receives the action (nobody can open it), so we use the passive can't + be + opened.
  18. My grandfather ___ (can't + open) jars anymore because his hands are weak.
    Accepted answers: can't open / cannot open
    My grandfather is the one trying to open the jars, so the verb is active: can't + open.
  19. The prize money ___ (ought to + divide) equally among the players, but the captain wants a bigger share.
    Answer: ought to be divided
    The money receives the action and the sentence is about now, so the passive is ought to + be + divided.
  20. Last season's prize money ___ (ought to + divide) equally, but the captain kept most of it.
    Answer: ought to have been divided
    The sentence is about last season, so we use the past passive modal: ought to have been + past participle.
  21. Rental cars ___ (have to + return) with a full tank of gas.
    Answer: have to be returned
    Customers return the cars; the cars receive the action, so we use have to + be + past participle.
  22. Omar ___ (have to + return) his cousin's camping gear before Friday.
    Answer: has to return
    Omar does the returning, so the verb is active, and have to becomes has to after a singular subject.
  23. If the airline loses your suitcase, it ___ (will + have to + pay) for everything inside.
    Accepted answers: will have to pay / 'll have to pay
    This is a future result in an if-sentence, so will goes before have to. The airline does the paying, so the verb is active.
  24. If my sales numbers stay high, I ___ (may + offer) a promotion next year.
    Answer: may be offered
    The speaker would receive the offer, so the passive may + be + offered is needed.
  25. The university ___ (may + offer) Hana a scholarship for her second year.
    Answer: may offer
    The university makes the offer and Hana receives it, so the verb is active: may offer.
  26. The concert sold out in minutes. The best seats ___ (may + already + offer) to fan club members before the public sale began.
    Accepted answers: may already have been offered / may have already been offered
    The seats possibly received the action at an earlier time, so we use the past passive modal may have been offered; already can go after may or after have.
  27. The theater ___ (may + already + offer) the best seats to fan club members before the public sale began.
    Accepted answers: may already have offered / may have already offered
    The theater possibly did the offering at an earlier time, so the verb is the active past modal may have offered; already can go after may or after have.
  28. The new bridge ___ (had better + finish) before winter, or drivers will face long detours.
    Answer: had better be finished
    The bridge is the thing being finished, so we use the passive had better + be + past participle.
  29. If you want to go to the concert on Saturday, you ___ (had better + finish) your essay tonight.
    Accepted answers: had better finish / 'd better finish
    You are the one who must do the work, so the active had better + simple form is correct.
  30. According to the instructions, the blood sample ___ (be supposed to + send) to the lab within 24 hours.
    Answer: is supposed to be sent
    The sample is the thing being sent, so we use be supposed to + be + past participle; is matches the singular subject.
  31. The invoice ___ (should + send) to the client last month, but someone forgot.
    Answer: should have been sent
    Last month shows something that should have happened in the past but didn't, so we use should have been + past participle.
  32. Kenji ___ (must + surprise) by the news yesterday. He didn't say a word for a whole minute.
    Answer: must have been surprised
    Yesterday shows a past moment, so this guess about his feelings uses must have been + past participle.
  33. Look at Grace's face! She ___ (must + surprise) to see all her old friends here.
    Answer: must be surprised
    This is a guess about how Grace feels right now, so we use the present passive modal must be surprised.
  34. Helmets ___ (must + wear) at all times on the construction site.
    Answer: must be worn
    People wear the helmets; the helmets receive the action, so the passive must be worn is correct.
  35. Every visitor ___ (must + wear) a helmet on the construction site.
    Answer: must wear
    Every visitor does the wearing and a helmet is the object, so the verb is active: must wear.
  36. Thousands of trees ___ (will + save) by the city's new recycling program.
    Answer: will be saved
    The trees receive the action and the program does it, so the passive will be saved is needed.
  37. The city's new recycling program ___ (will + save) thousands of trees.
    Answer: will save
    The program does the saving and trees is the object, so the verb is active: will save.
  38. Job seekers ___ (can + look for) openings on the city's website every day.
    Answer: can look for
    Job seekers do the looking, so the active form can look for is correct.
  39. Bus schedules ___ (can + find) on the transit company's app.
    Answer: can be found
    People find the schedules; the schedules receive the action, so we use can + be + found.
  40. Gardeners ___ (should + test) the soil before they plant vegetables.
    Answer: should test
    Gardeners do the testing and the soil is the object, so the active should test is correct.
  41. Car batteries ___ (should + test) before the start of every winter.
    Answer: should be tested
    Someone tests the batteries; the batteries receive the action, so we use should be tested.
  42. My new cleaning spray ___ (may + kill) my basil plant last week. Its leaves turned black the next day.
    Answer: may have killed
    This is a possible past event, and the spray did the killing, so we use the active past modal may have killed.
  43. Most of the bacteria ___ (may + kill) when the water was boiled.
    Answer: may have been killed
    The bacteria possibly received the action at a past time, so we use the past passive modal may have been killed.
  44. The dinosaurs ___ (may + die) out because of a huge asteroid.
    Answer: may have died
    Die has no object, so it cannot be passive. The past possibility is may have died.
  45. These reports ___ (had better + deliver) to the client by Friday, or we'll lose the contract.
    Answer: had better be delivered
    Someone delivers the reports; the reports receive the action, so we use had better + be + delivered.
  46. Our team ___ (had better + receive) the new laptops before Monday, or we can't start the project.
    Answer: had better receive
    Our team does the receiving and the laptops are the object, so the verb is active: had better receive.
  47. A powerful storm ___ the coast of Florida last night.
    The storm does the action and the coast is the object, so the active hit is right. Was hit can't be followed by an object here.
  48. Several houses on our street ___ by falling trees.
    The houses receive the action and the trees come after by, so the passive were hit is needed.
  49. A falling tree ___ two parked cars during the storm.
    The tree does the action and the cars are the object, so the active hit is needed. Was hit can't be followed by an object here.
  50. The way people shop ___ forever by the internet.
    The by-phrase names what did the action, so the verb must be passive: was changed.
  51. Her whole life ___ by a single phone call.
    Change can be active without an object (Her life changed), but a by-phrase needs the passive: was changed by.
  52. The speech ___ by a short question-and-answer session.
    The session came after the speech and is named after by, so the passive was followed is needed. Followed by without be is not a complete verb.
  53. The concert ___ by a huge fireworks show.
    The fireworks show came after the concert and is named after by, so the passive was followed is right.
  54. Loud cheers ___ the winning goal.
    The cheers came after the goal: the cheers do the action and the goal is the object, so the active followed is needed. Were followed can't take an object here.
  55. A long silence ___ the announcement.
    The silence came after the announcement, which is the object, so the active followed is right.
  56. Two swimmers ___ out to sea by a strong current.
    The swimmers receive the action and the current comes after by, so the passive were swept is needed.
  57. Many beach chairs ___ away by the high waves.
    The chairs receive the action of the waves, so the passive were swept is right.
  58. The strong wind ___ all the leaves off our porch.
    The wind does the action and the leaves are the object, so the active swept is right.
  59. More than 100 people ___ in the heat wave last summer.
    Die has no object, so it is never passive: died.
  60. Many of the trees in the park ___ during the long drought.
    Die is intransitive, so were died is wrong. Use the active: died.
  61. Three hikers ___ in an avalanche last winter.
    Kill needs an object in the active. The hikers receive the action, so the passive were killed is needed.
  62. Dozens of birds ___ when they flew into the glass tower.
    The birds receive the action, and kill needs an object in the active, so the passive were killed is needed.
  63. The flooding ___ by better drainage systems.
    The flooding receives the action and the drainage systems come after by, so the past passive modal could have been lessened is needed.
  64. Earlier treatment ___ the patient's pain.
    Treatment does the action and the pain is the object, so the active past modal could have lessened is right.
  65. A stronger sea wall ___ the damage to the harbor.
    The sea wall would do the action and the damage is the object, so the active could have lessened is needed. The passive can't be followed by an object here.
  66. A similar program already ___ in several European countries.
    Exist has no object, so it is never passive: exists.
  67. Nobody knows if water ___ on that distant planet.
    Exist is intransitive, so is existed is wrong. Use the active: exists.
