Cambridge Grammar for IELTS Unit 23 – Linking Ideas

Choose or type the answer to each question, then press Check to see if you are right.
  1. The campus library was closed for repairs, so ___
    So introduces the result of the first clause. Studying somewhere else is a result of the library being closed, while a leaking roof would be the reason for the closure, which needs because, not so.
  2. If you want to cut your transportation costs, you could buy a monthly bus pass. Alternatively, ___
    Alternatively introduces another possible choice with the same aim. Riding a bike is another way to cut transportation costs, but taking a taxi every day would raise them, so it is not an alternative.
  3. In Japan, people usually take off their shoes before they enter a home. Likewise, ___
    Likewise introduces an idea that is similar to the one before. Leaving shoes at the door in Korea matches the Japanese custom, while keeping shoes on indoors is a contrast, which would need in contrast or however.
  4. The company did not invest in new technology for many years. Consequently, ___
    Consequently introduces a result. Losing customers is a natural result of not investing in technology, but becoming the most modern firm is the opposite of what would follow.
  5. Cooking at home is usually cheaper than eating out. Furthermore, ___
    Furthermore adds another point that supports the same argument. Choosing healthier ingredients is a second advantage of cooking at home, while losing free time is a disadvantage, which would need however.
  6. In spite of the fact that the hiking trail is steep and rocky, ___
    In spite of the fact that introduces a contrast, so the second clause must be surprising after the first. Families completing a steep, rocky trail is unexpected, but walkers turning back is exactly what we would expect.
  7. Tap water in our city is cheap and safe to drink. In contrast, ___
    In contrast introduces something different from or opposite to the previous idea. Expensive bottled water contrasts with cheap tap water, while regular testing is just more information about tap water.
  8. Only one in ten students who sign up for the free online course actually finishes it. In other words, ___
    In other words introduces the same idea said in a different way. Most students not completing the course restates the first sentence, while good course design is a new idea.
  9. Our hotel was right in the center of the city, yet ___
    Yet introduces a contrasting, surprising idea, like but. A quiet room is surprising in a hotel in the city center, but being close to the sights is expected, so it does not contrast.
  10. Visitors are not allowed to enter the laboratory because ___
    Because introduces the reason for the first clause. Dangerous chemicals explain why visitors are kept out, but opening hours do not explain why entry is not allowed.
  11. My doctor says that although I exercise regularly, ___
    Although introduces a contrast between two clauses. High blood pressure is surprising for someone who exercises regularly, while being in good shape is what we would expect, so there is no contrast.
  12. The outdoor concert has been postponed due to the fact that ___
    Due to the fact that introduces a reason, followed by a full clause. Storms explain why the concert was postponed, while telling fans to check the website is advice, not a reason.
  13. Some people believe that building more roads will solve traffic problems. ___, research suggests that new roads often attract even more cars.
    However introduces an idea that contrasts with the previous sentence. The research goes against the belief that more roads solve traffic problems, but in addition would add a point that supports it.
  14. Public museums should be free for several reasons. ___, free entry encourages people from all backgrounds to visit.
    Firstly introduces the first point in a list, here the first of several reasons. Alternatively introduces a different option or choice, not the first reason in an argument.
  15. Online courses allow students to study at their own pace. ___, they save learners the time and cost of traveling to a campus.
    Furthermore adds another point that supports the previous one. Saving travel time and cost is another advantage of online courses, but in contrast would need an opposite idea.
  16. Electric buses produce no exhaust fumes and are ___ better for the air quality in busy cities.
    Therefore shows that an idea is a result of the one before, and it can go after the verb be. Cleaner air is a result of producing no fumes, while nevertheless would signal a surprising contrast that is not there.
  17. Firstly, a tourist tax would discourage visitors. Secondly, it would hurt small family-run hotels. ___, it would be expensive to collect.
    Thirdly introduces the third point in a list after firstly and secondly. Similarly introduces an idea that is like the previous one, but here the writer is simply giving the next reason.
  18. ___ the new shopping mall has created hundreds of jobs, many local residents are unhappy about the extra traffic.
    Although is a conjunction, so it can be followed by a subject and verb (the new shopping mall has created). Despite is a preposition and must be followed by a noun or -ing form, not a full clause.
  19. In my view, schools should teach children how to manage money. ___, banks could offer free workshops for teenagers on saving and budgeting.
    In addition adds a further suggestion to the previous one. Consequently would mean the bank workshops are a result of schools teaching money skills, which is not the case.
  20. The city added hundreds of new bike lanes last year, and the number of cyclists has doubled ___.
    As a result can go at the end of a clause to show that it describes a result, here of the new bike lanes. Moreover adds information and goes at the beginning of a sentence or clause, not at the end.
  21. Recycling rates in our city have improved a great deal in recent years. ___, too much plastic still ends up in landfills.
    Nevertheless introduces a contrast: something is still true despite the previous idea. Plastic still going to landfills contrasts with better recycling, while and would simply add information.
  22. Learning a second language can improve your career prospects. ___, it can make traveling abroad much easier.
    Moreover adds another point that supports the previous one. Easier travel is a second benefit of learning a language, not a contrast (however) or a result of better career prospects (as a result).
  23. Many processed foods and drinks contain a surprising amount of hidden sugar. ___, a single can of soda may contain around ten teaspoons.
    For example introduces an example of the general idea before it. A can of soda is one example of a product with hidden sugar; it is not a result of the first sentence or a contrast with it.
  24. Young people leave rural areas for a number of reasons. ___, most universities and large employers are based in cities.
    Firstly introduces the first point in a list, here the first of several reasons. However would need a contrast, and as a result would wrongly present where universities are as a result of young people leaving.
  25. Many universities now offer degree programs entirely online. ___, a growing number of high schools let students take some classes from home.
    Similarly introduces a situation that is like the one before. High schools offering classes from home match universities teaching online, so however, which needs a contrast, does not fit, and firstly would need a list of points.
  26. Social media helps people stay in touch with friends who live far away. ___, spending too much time online can make some users feel lonely.
    However introduces a contrasting idea. Feeling lonely is a negative point after a positive one, so moreover, which adds a point in the same direction, does not fit.
  27. My parents think a gap year is a waste of time, ___ I see it as a valuable chance to gain work experience.
    But joins two contrasting clauses in one sentence. The writer's view is the opposite of the parents' view, so because, which gives a reason, does not fit.
  28. I chose to study nursing ___ I have always wanted a job that helps people.
    Because introduces the reason for something in the same sentence. Wanting to help people is the reason for choosing nursing, not a contrast, so but does not fit, and however cannot join two clauses like this.
  29. The town's largest factory closed last year, and hundreds of workers lost their jobs. ___, many families have moved away to look for work in other cities.
    As a result introduces the result of the previous situation. Families moving away to find work follows from the job losses, so however, which needs a contrast, does not fit, and the move is not an example of the factory closing.
  30. Rewrite the sentence using although: Despite the drop in crime over the past decade, many residents feel unsafe walking home at night. → ___
    Although is a conjunction, so it needs a clause with a subject and verb: there has been a drop. It cannot be followed by a noun phrase alone, and it is never used together with but.
  31. Rewrite the sentence using because: The heating in our school broke down, so we had to wear our coats in class. → ___
    Because introduces the reason, so it goes before the broken heating, which caused the coats. The first option puts because before the result, and because and so are never used together to join the same two clauses.
  32. Rewrite the sentence using due to: Train fares have risen sharply, so more commuters are now driving to work. → More commuters are now driving to work ___ train fares have risen sharply.
    Answer: due to the fact that
    Due to is a preposition, so before a full clause (train fares have risen) we need due to the fact that. Due to on its own is followed only by a noun phrase, such as due to higher fares.
  33. Rewrite the sentence using as a result: Many young people cannot afford to buy a home because house prices have doubled in ten years. → ___
    As a result begins the sentence that describes the result, so the cause (the rise in house prices) must come first. Reversing the sentences makes young people's problem the cause of the price rise, and as a result of must be followed by a noun, not a clause.
  34. Rewrite the sentence using but: Although the city has built three new parks, many neighborhoods still have no green space nearby. → ___
    But goes between two contrasting clauses, not at the start of the first one as although can. Although and but are never used together to join the same two clauses.
  35. Rewrite the sentence using however: Although electric cars are cheap to run, they are still expensive to buy. → ___
    However links two separate sentences and usually starts the second one, followed by a comma. It cannot introduce a clause the way although does, and a clause starting with although cannot stand alone as a sentence.
  36. Rewrite the sentence using in spite of: Although the players trained every day for a month, they still lost the final. → In spite of ___ the players trained every day for a month, they still lost the final.
    Answer: the fact that
    In spite of is a preposition, so before a full clause (the players trained) we need in spite of the fact that. In spite of on its own is followed by a noun or -ing form, such as in spite of training every day.
