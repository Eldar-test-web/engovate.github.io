Oxford Practice Grammar Advanced Chapter 6 – Articles (A/An or The)

Choose or type the answer to each question, then press Check to see if you are right.
  1. In "Last night I watched a documentary about a farmer who keeps bees on city rooftops," why is "a" used before "documentary"?
    A/an introduces something new to the listener. The documentary hasn't been mentioned before; later it would be the documentary.
  2. In "Kate bought a lamp and a rug last week, but she's returning the lamp because it's too bright," why is "the" used before "lamp"?
    The lamp has already been mentioned (a lamp), so the listener knows which one is meant and the is used. It is one particular lamp, not lamps in general.
  3. In "That building with the glass dome is a library, not a museum," why is "a" used before "library"?
    A library puts the building into a category: it says what kind of building it is. It doesn't refer to a library the listener already knows.
  4. In "Can I borrow a charger? My phone is almost dead," why is "a" used before "charger"?
    The speaker doesn't mean a particular charger; any charger will do, so a is used. The would mean a charger both people already know about.
  5. In "My uncle is a pilot, so he's hardly ever home on holidays," why is "a" used before "pilot"?
    A pilot classifies the uncle by his job, and a singular job noun after be needs a/an: he is a pilot. Being a pilot is work, not a belief.
  6. In "My sister became a Buddhist after spending a year in Thailand," why is "a" used before "Buddhist"?
    A Buddhist classifies the sister by her religious beliefs, so a/an is used. Buddhist names what she believes, not what job she does.
  7. In "In geometry, a square is a rectangle with four equal sides," why is "a" used before "rectangle"?
    The sentence defines a square by putting it into a wider group, so a/an is used for the category: a rectangle. Rectangle is a common noun, not a proper noun.
  8. In "Our new apartment has a huge balcony but a tiny kitchen," why is "a" used before "huge balcony"?
    A huge balcony describes one particular feature of the apartment, so a is used. It isn't a definition of what an apartment is.
  9. In "My first phone was a Nokia, and it almost never broke," why is "a" used before "Nokia"?
    A Nokia means one phone made by Nokia. A/an can go before a company or artist's name to mean one example of what they make.
  10. In "Can you put the milk back in the fridge when you're done?" why is "the" used before "fridge"?
    The fridge is one of the ordinary things at home that both speakers know, so it is treated as known and takes the. It isn't part of nature, like the sky or the sea.
  11. In "The moon looked enormous as it rose over the hills last night," why is "the" used before "moon"?
    The moon is part of the physical world that everyone shares, so it is treated as known and takes the, like the sun and the sky.
  12. In "The car is making a strange noise, so I'll call the mechanic tomorrow," why is "the" used before "mechanic"?
    The mechanic is the person whose job is fixing our car, the one we normally use, so the identifies them by their job. There are many mechanics, so it isn't a unique role.
  13. In "Crowds lined the streets hoping to catch a glimpse of the president," why is "the" used before "president"?
    A country has only one president at a time, so the president identifies a person by a unique role in society.
  14. In "When the smoke alarm went off, a neighbor called the fire department," why is "the" used before "fire department"?
    The fire department is a professional organization, like the police or the army, and these take the. It is a group of people, not one person with a unique role.
  15. In "My grandfather taught himself to play the violin when he was sixty," why is "the" used before "violin"?
    With play, a musical instrument takes the because we mean the instrument in general, not one particular violin.
  16. In "The tiger is now an endangered species in most of Asia," why is "the" used before "tiger"?
    The tiger means tigers as a species, not one tiger, so the is used to make a generalization about the whole group.
  17. In "Our final exams are always at the end of the semester," why is "the" used before "end"?
    End is identified by the phrase of the semester that follows it, so the is used. There is no relative clause (with who, which or that) after end.
  18. In "The bus that goes to the airport leaves every twenty minutes," why is "the" used before "bus"?
    The relative clause that goes to the airport shows exactly which bus is meant, so the is used. No of-phrase follows bus.
  19. In "Lucy was the fastest swimmer on her team last year," why is "the" used before "fastest swimmer"?
    A superlative like fastest picks out one person from a group, so it is used with the. On her team is not an of-phrase or a relative clause.
  20. ___ Great Lakes hold about a fifth of the world's fresh surface water.
    Plural names of groups of lakes, islands or mountains take the: the Great Lakes, the Andes. A can't be used with a plural noun.
  21. For the science fair, our team built ___ set of six small robots.
    The set is mentioned for the first time, so a is used. The would suggest a set the listener already knows about, and set is countable, so it needs an article.
  22. Every guest room comes with a basket of ___ fresh towels.
    After a group word like a basket of, a plural noun used in a general way has no article. A can't go before a plural noun.
  23. Ferries cross ___ Strait of Gibraltar between Spain and Morocco many times a day.
    Names of seas, oceans, rivers and straits take the: the Strait of Gibraltar, the Pacific. There is only one, so a is wrong.
  24. Our cabin is on ___ northern shore of the lake.
    The northern shore is identified by the of-phrase after it (of the lake), and a lake has only one northern shore, so the is used.
  25. Our neighbors moved from Chile to ___ Canada when their kids were small.
    Most country names, like Canada, are proper nouns with no article.
  26. The United States bought Alaska from ___ Russia in 1867.
    Russia is a country name, and most country names have no article. Only names with a word like states or kingdom, such as the United States, take the.
  27. ___ Garcias next door have invited us to their barbecue on Saturday.
    A family name in the plural, meaning the whole family, is a plural proper noun and takes the: the Garcias.
  28. The printing press reached England in ___ fifteenth century.
    Centuries are named with the + ordinal number: in the fifteenth century. There is only one fifteenth century, so a is wrong.
  29. Prague is the capital of ___ Czech Republic.
    Country names that include a common noun such as republic, kingdom or states take the: the Czech Republic, the United Kingdom.
  30. Her father was ___ Italian architect, one of many who worked on the new airport.
    He is classified as one of many Italian architects, so the indefinite article is used, and an comes before the vowel sound of Italian. The would mean the only one.
  31. Dr. Patel is a surgeon, and she is also ___ talented painter.
    A classifies Dr. Patel by something she does, and a singular countable noun after be needs a/an. The would mean one painter we already know about.
  32. Several of the world's tallest buildings are in ___ UAE.
    Short forms of country names with a word like states, kingdom or emirates take the: the UAE, the US.
  33. This is ___ cheapest flight I could find for the holidays.
    A superlative such as cheapest picks out one thing from a group, so it takes the.
  34. He is one of the leading experts on ___ classical music.
    Classical music is an uncountable noun used here in a general sense, so it has no article.
  35. As ___ teenager, he spent every summer working on his uncle's farm.
    As + a singular countable noun describes the role or stage someone was in, so a is needed: as a teenager. The would mean one particular teenager.
  36. Our neighbor's dog is a huge poodle with ___ pink collar.
    A is used in descriptions of particular features: a poodle with a pink collar. The collar hasn't been mentioned before, so the is wrong.
  37. The old teapot has ___ chipped handle, but it still pours perfectly.
    A chipped handle describes one particular feature of the teapot, so a is used. The handle isn't being identified as one we already know about.
  38. The statue shows a young girl in a long dress and ___ sandals.
    Sandals is a plural noun mentioned for the first time in a description, so it has no article. A can't be used with a plural noun.
  39. On weekends he wears ___ baggy shirts and old jeans.
    Baggy shirts is a plural noun used to describe the kind of clothes he wears, not particular shirts we know about, so it has no article.
  40. The new manager talks to customers in ___ very friendly way.
    In a ... way describes how someone does something, and way is a singular countable noun, so a is used.
  41. A: Have you ever ridden a horse? B: No, this is ___ first time I've been on one.
    First, like a superlative, picks out one occasion, so it takes the: the first time.
  42. A: Is a peanut really a nut? B: No, it's ___ legume, like peas and beans.
    In a definition, a/an puts something into a category: it's a legume. The would wrongly suggest one particular legume.
  43. A: Where did you park the car? B: It's at ___ far end of the parking lot.
    The far end is identified by the of-phrase after it (of the parking lot), and there is only one, so the is used.
  44. A: Why is the sand so hot? B: It's been in ___ sun all afternoon.
    The sun is part of the physical world that everyone knows, so it takes the.
  45. A: Why is Nick taking so many chemistry classes? B: He wants to be ___ pharmacist one day.
    A/an classifies someone by the work they do: to be a pharmacist. The would mean one particular pharmacist.
  46. A: Is your cousin still working at the bank? B: No, she joined ___ air force last year.
    Professional organizations such as the army, the navy and the air force take the. An would mean any air force, not her country's.
  47. Excuse me, there's ___ fly in my soup!
    There's introduces something new, so a singular countable noun after it takes a/an, not the.
  48. Many tourists go diving in ___ Maldives.
    Plural names of island groups take the: the Maldives, the Bahamas.
  49. Their son Alexander is nicknamed ___ Sasha by the whole family.
    People's names and nicknames are proper nouns with no article.
  50. I've walked through the whole train, but I can't find ___ free seat anywhere.
    The speaker means any free seat, not a particular one, so a is used. The would mean a seat both speakers already know about, and seat is countable, so it needs an article.
  51. A stray cat appeared on our porch last winter, and now ___ cat sleeps on our sofa every night.
    The cat has already been mentioned (a stray cat), so the second time it is known and takes the. A would mean a different cat.
  52. We spent a week on ___ Maui, one of the islands of Hawaii.
    The name of a single island usually has no article, unlike plural names of island groups such as the Maldives.
  53. Tests showed that the snake in the backyard belonged to ___ harmless species, so nobody needed to panic.
    A classifies the snake as belonging to one of many harmless species. The would mean a species already identified, and species here is a singular countable noun, so it needs an article.
  54. ___ city of Kyoto has more than 1,600 temples.
    In the pattern the + city/island/state + of + name, the is used before the common noun: the city of Kyoto.
  55. Last summer we drove along the coast of ___ Portugal.
    Portugal is a country name, which has no article, even after of.
  56. Two firefighters rescued a kitten from a storm drain yesterday. Afterward, ___ firefighters both said it was the smallest animal they had ever saved.
    The firefighters have already been mentioned, so the refers back to them. With no article they would sound like other, unknown firefighters, which doesn't fit with both.
  57. Our office manager wants to buy ___ coffee machine for the break room.
    The coffee machine is mentioned for the first time and no particular one has been chosen, so a is used.
  58. Let's ask ___ Professor Kim whether the lab is open on Sunday.
    A title before a person's name, such as Professor Kim or Dr. Lee, takes no article.
  59. Correct the words in square brackets: Getting locked out of my hotel room in my pajamas was [really awkward experience].
    Answer: a really awkward experience
    Experience here means one event, so it is a singular countable noun and needs a/an: a really awkward experience.
  60. Correct the words in square brackets: My grandparents decided to study [the Spanish] before they retired to Mexico.
    Answer: Spanish
    Names of languages have no article: study Spanish. The Spanish means the people of Spain.
  61. Correct the words in square brackets: My parents met while they were both working in [the Kenya].
    Answer: Kenya
    Most country names, like Kenya, have no article.
  62. Correct the words in square brackets: My grandmother is always baking [the cookies] for the kids on her street.
    Answer: cookies
    Cookies is a plural noun used in a general way, not particular cookies we already know about, so it has no article.
  63. Correct the words in square brackets: Someone had painted [words] 'No Parking' across our garage door.
    Answer: the words
    The words are identified by the quotation that follows ('No Parking'), so the is needed.
  64. Correct the words in square brackets: I left my keys on [kitchen table] this morning.
    Answer: the kitchen table
    The kitchen table is an ordinary thing in our home that everyone knows about, so this singular countable noun needs the.
  65. Correct the words in square brackets: When the interviewer asked why I wanted the job, I couldn't answer [question] at first.
    Answer: the question
    The question has just been mentioned (why I wanted the job), so it is known and needs the.
  66. Correct the words in square brackets: My surname has an I in the middle, but the airline changed the I to [the O] on my boarding pass.
    Accepted answers: O / an O
    The O hasn't been mentioned before, so the is wrong; we say O, or an O because the letter O begins with a vowel sound.
  67. Correct the words in square brackets: Bringing an umbrella turned out to be [good idea], because it poured all afternoon.
    Answer: a good idea
    Idea is a singular countable noun, so it needs a/an: a good idea.
  68. Correct the words in square brackets: The printer had added an extra zero, so the clerk corrected [number] by hand.
    Answer: the number
    The number is the particular one with the extra zero, which is already known, so this singular countable noun needs the.
  69. Correct the words in square brackets: We repainted the living room last month, and everyone loves [new color].
    Accepted answers: the new color / the new colour
    The new color is the particular color of the repainted room, already known from the context, so the is needed.
  70. Correct the words in square brackets: He answered every question from the panel with [the complete honesty].
    Answer: complete honesty
    Honesty is an uncountable abstract noun used in a general way after with, so it has no article.
  71. Correct the words in square brackets: The teacher shook her head in [the disbelief] when she saw my test score.
    Answer: disbelief
    In disbelief uses an abstract noun in a general way to describe how someone reacted, so there is no article.
  72. Correct the words in square brackets: The team was losing, so the coach decided they needed [second striker].
    Answer: a second striker
    Second here means one more, so it takes a: a second striker. A singular countable noun can't be used without an article.
  73. Correct the words in square brackets: The new password must have [the X] or another unusual letter in it.
    Answer: an X
    No particular X is meant, so the is wrong; an is used because the letter X begins with a vowel sound (ex).
  74. Correct the words in square brackets: The waiter handed back my card with [the wink].
    Answer: a wink
    A wink is a single action mentioned for the first time, so it takes a, not the.
  75. Correct the words in square brackets: Every morning we're woken up by [wonderful smell] of fresh bread from the bakery downstairs.
    Answer: the wonderful smell
    The smell is identified by the of-phrase that follows it (of fresh bread), so the is needed.
  76. Correct the words in square brackets: After the exam was over, I felt a huge sense of [the relief].
    Answer: relief
    Relief is an uncountable abstract noun used here in a general way, so there is no article after sense of.
