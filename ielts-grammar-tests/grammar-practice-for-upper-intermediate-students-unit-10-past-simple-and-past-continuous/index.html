Grammar Practice for Upper Intermediate Students Unit 10 – Past Simple and Past Continuous

Choose or type the answer to each question, then press Check to see if you are right.
  1. In 1998, Lagos ___ (be) already one of the biggest cities in Africa.
    Answer: was
    Be is a stative verb, so a situation at a past time takes the past simple, not the past continuous.
  2. It ___ (be) almost midnight when our train finally pulled into Boston.
    Answer: was
    Be describes the situation at that moment, and as a stative verb it takes the past simple.
  3. It was chaos at the airport: babies ___ (scream), phones were ringing, and nobody knew when the flights would leave.
    Answer: were screaming
    The past continuous describes the background scene, actions in progress at that time, like were ringing.
  4. On the last day of school, the kids in the hallway were laughing and ___ (shout) as they ran outside.
    Accepted answers: shouting / were shouting
    Both actions were in progress at the same time, so both are past continuous; after and, were can be left out before the second -ing verb.
  5. The children at the zoo ___ (want) to see the baby elephant more than anything else.
    Answer: wanted
    Want is a stative verb, so it is not normally used in the continuous; the past simple describes the wish at that time.
  6. A stranger at the bus stop offered me a bottle of cold water because sweat ___ (pour) down my face.
    Answer: was pouring
    The sweating was in progress when the stranger offered the water, so the past continuous is used.
  7. When Theo walked in from the storm, his coat ___ (drip) wet, so I handed him a towel and a hanger.
    Answer: was dripping
    The coat was already dripping when he walked in, an action in progress, so the past continuous is used.
  8. When I got home at midnight, the music from the party upstairs ___ (thump) through the ceiling.
    Answer: was thumping
    The music had started earlier and was still going on when I got home, so the past continuous fits.
  9. As a teenager, Grace ___ (love) old black-and-white movies.
    Answer: loved
    Love is a stative verb, so a feeling in the past takes the past simple, not the past continuous.
  10. Whenever Omar spoke Spanish with his grandmother, he always ___ (feel) closer to his family's history.
    Answer: felt
    With always and whenever, the past simple describes what happened each time; feel here is also a state.
  11. Halfway through the hike, Lena ___ (decide) to turn back because of the dark clouds.
    Answer: decided
    Deciding is a single completed action at one moment, so it takes the past simple.
  12. As soon as the teacher ___ (start) the video, the students went quiet.
    Answer: started
    In a sequence of completed events, both verbs take the past simple: first the video started, then the students went quiet.
  13. When I opened the oven door, the smoke alarm ___ (go) off.
    Answer: went
    The alarm went off right after the door opened, a completed event in a sequence, so the past simple is used.
  14. By the time we got to the street party, our neighbors ___ (all/dance) in the middle of the road.
    Answer: were all dancing
    The dancing was in progress when we arrived, so the past continuous is used; all goes after were.
  15. When the last runner crossed the finish line, her teammates were cheering and ___ (clap).
    Accepted answers: clapping / were clapping
    Both actions were in progress at that moment, so both are past continuous; after and, were can be left out before the second -ing verb.
  16. As soon as Hannah ___ (leave) the office on Friday, she turned off her work phone.
    Answer: left
    Leaving is a completed action at a point in time, and the next action follows it, so it takes the past simple.
  17. After the twelve-hour flight, my little brother ___ (be) too tired to eat dinner.
    Answer: was
    Be is a stative verb, so a state in the past takes the past simple.
  18. When the hikers ___ (get back) to the parking lot, it was already dark.
    Answer: got back
    Getting back is a completed action at a point in time, so the when clause takes the past simple.
  19. Ana ___ (open) the envelope and read the letter twice before she said a word.
    Answer: opened
    This is a series of completed actions one after another, so each one takes the past simple.
  20. I looked out the window and ___ (see) that it had snowed during the night.
    Answer: saw
    Seeing is a completed action that followed looking out, so it takes the past simple; see is not normally used in the continuous.
  21. When I came out of the dentist's office, my kids ___ (wait) for me in the car.
    Answer: were waiting
    The waiting began before I came out and was still in progress, so the past continuous is used.
  22. The last person to leave the lab ___ (lock) all the cabinets and turned off the lights.
    Answer: locked
    Two completed actions in a sequence both take the past simple, like turned off.
  23. When the dog ran into the street, Ben ___ (shout) its name as loud as he could.
    Answer: shouted
    Shouting was a completed reaction to the dog running off, so it takes the past simple, not the past continuous.
  24. I waited at the bus stop for forty minutes, but no bus ___ (come).
    Answer: came
    The past simple describes a completed event; here it says the event did not happen during that time.
  25. Before the race, Carlos ___ (tie) his shoelaces in double knots so they wouldn't come loose.
    Answer: tied
    Tying the laces is a single completed action, so it takes the past simple.
  26. For the party game, the kids ___ (blindfold) the birthday girl and spun her around three times.
    Answer: blindfolded
    This is a completed action followed by another one (spun), so the past simple is used.
  27. The medicine tasted so awful that my son ___ (gag) on the first spoonful.
    Answer: gagged
    Gagging was a single completed reaction, so the past simple is used; the final g doubles before -ed.
  28. From our hotel room, we ___ (can hear) the waves breaking on the beach.
    Answer: could hear
    The past of can is could; can hear describes what we were able to hear, and it has no continuous form.
  29. When I walked into the kitchen, my dad ___ (talk) to someone on the phone in Korean.
    Answer: was talking
    The phone call was already in progress when I walked in, so the past continuous is used.
  30. When I tuned in to the radio, the announcer ___ (say) something about road closures, but I missed the end of it.
    Answer: was saying
    The past continuous shows the speech was in progress and I heard only part of it, not the complete event.
  31. Through the thin wall, I could hear our neighbor on the phone. He ___ (say) that he was moving to Texas when his voice suddenly dropped to a whisper.
    Answer: was saying
    The speaking was in progress when his voice dropped, so the past continuous describes it.
  32. Back in 2015, our team ___ (deserve) to win the final, but the referee made some terrible calls.
    Answer: deserved
    Deserve is a stative verb, so it takes the past simple, not the past continuous.
  33. Our coach told reporters that we deserved to lose. ___ (deserve) to lose? We played our best game of the season!
    Answer: Deserved
    An echo question repeats the other person's verb in the same form to show surprise, so it is the past simple deserved.
  34. Last Saturday Leo said he'd be "a little late," but he showed up two hours after the party started. What ___ (he/mean) by "a little"?
    Answer: did he mean
    A past simple question uses did + subject + infinitive; mean is stative, so the continuous is not used.
  35. There were three police cars outside our building when I got home, and nobody could tell me what ___ (go on).
    Answer: was going on
    The situation was in progress at the time I got home, so the past continuous is used.
  36. The meeting had been moved to Room 12, but I waited in Room 4 for half an hour. Why ___ (not someone tell) me?
    Accepted answers: didn't someone tell / did someone not tell
    A negative question about a completed past event uses didn't + subject + infinitive.
  37. The smoke was so thick that the firefighters outside couldn't see what ___ (happen) inside the building.
    Answer: was happening
    The events inside were in progress at that time, so the what clause takes the past continuous.
  38. The accident ___ (happen) on a rainy night in November 2009.
    Answer: happened
    A completed event at a stated past time takes the past simple.
  39. The year I graduated, the economy ___ (be) in terrible shape, so jobs were hard to find.
    Answer: was
    Be is a stative verb, so a past situation takes the past simple.
  40. When the storm clouds appeared, we ___ (all/lie out) on the beach with our books.
    Answer: were all lying out
    Lying out was already in progress when the clouds appeared, so the past continuous is used; all goes after were, and lie becomes lying.
  41. When the lights went out, my roommate ___ (read) in bed.
    Answer: was reading
    The reading was in progress when the lights went out, so it takes the past continuous.
  42. I ___ (just/doze) in front of the TV when the doorbell rang.
    Answer: was just dozing
    The dozing was in progress when the doorbell interrupted it, so the past continuous is used; just goes after was.
  43. The twins ___ (look for) firewood near the campsite when they found an old silver coin.
    Answer: were looking for
    Looking for firewood was in progress when the find interrupted it, so the past continuous is used.
  44. We were just sitting down to dinner when my cousin ___ (arrive) from the airport.
    Answer: arrived
    The arrival is the short completed event that interrupted the dinner, so it takes the past simple.
  45. The math teacher I had in sixth grade ___ (be) a short woman with a loud laugh and bright red glasses.
    Answer: was
    Be describes a state, so a description in the past takes the past simple.
  46. The judge ___ (look) directly at the young man and asked him to stand up.
    Answer: looked
    Two completed actions one after the other both take the past simple.
  47. As soon as the sun set, the sky ___ (go) dark and the temperature dropped.
    Answer: went
    This is a completed change in a sequence of events, so the past simple is used.
  48. The store's doors ___ (open) at exactly nine o'clock on Black Friday, and hundreds of shoppers rushed inside.
    Answer: opened
    Opening is a completed event at a past time, followed by the next event, so it takes the past simple.
  49. When I first saw the building, I ___ (think) it was a church, but it was actually a library.
    Answer: thought
    Think meaning believe is a stative verb, so it takes the past simple.
  50. I thought you ___ (be) allergic to cats, so I was surprised to see you holding one.
    Answer: were
    After the past verb thought, the past simple were gives the belief at that time; be is stative.
  51. At the end of the class, the professor ___ (say) that the exam would be on Friday.
    Answer: said
    Saying is a completed action at a past time, so it takes the past simple.
  52. I ___ (phone) the clinic three times yesterday afternoon, but the line was always busy.
    Answer: phoned
    Completed actions at a finished past time (yesterday afternoon) take the past simple.
  53. I sent Priya a message at lunch, but she ___ (not answer) until late in the evening.
    Accepted answers: didn't answer / did not answer
    The negative past simple is didn't + infinitive, used here for a completed event.
  54. I saw your car outside the library at midnight. What ___ (you/do) there so late?
    Answer: were you doing
    The question asks about an activity in progress at a particular past time, so the past continuous is used.
  55. In the middle of the night, I ___ (hear) a strange noise in the attic.
    Answer: heard
    Hear is not normally used in the continuous, and a single noise is a completed event, so the past simple is used.
  56. I ___ (have) breakfast when the news about the earthquake came on TV.
    Answer: was having
    Have here means eat, an activity, so it can be continuous; breakfast was in progress when the news came on.
  57. The bus was so crowded that I ___ (can not) find a seat.
    Accepted answers: couldn't / could not
    The past of can is could, so the negative in the past is couldn't.
  58. Sorry I missed your call earlier. What ___ (you/want) to ask me when you called?
    Answer: did you want
    Want is a stative verb, so the past simple question form did you want is used.
  59. The city workers ___ (clear out) the blocked drains when the heavy rain started.
    Answer: were clearing out
    The work was in progress when the rain started, so the past continuous is used.
  60. I still wear the watch that my grandfather ___ (give) me for my sixteenth birthday.
    Answer: gave
    Giving the watch was a completed event at a past time, so it takes the past simple.
  61. On the drive to Denver, we ___ (come across) a small town with a fantastic diner.
    Answer: came across
    Coming across something is a completed event, so it takes the past simple.
  62. The packages ___ (be addressed) to our old apartment, so they took weeks to reach us.
    Answer: were addressed
    This is the past simple passive, were + past participle, giving a fact about the packages at that time.
  63. After the camping trip, our clothes ___ (all/smell) of smoke.
    Answer: all smelled
    Smell meaning have a smell is a stative verb, so it takes the past simple; all goes before the verb, and US English uses smelled.
  64. On the morning of the exam, Nina ___ (wake up) an hour before her alarm.
    Answer: woke up
    A completed event at a past time takes the past simple; wake is irregular and becomes woke.
  65. The moment I ___ (wake up), I knew something was wrong; the house was too quiet.
    Answer: woke up
    A time clause about a completed past event takes the past simple.
  66. Ruth stood very still and ___ (listen) for the sound of a car in the driveway.
    Answer: listened
    This is a completed action that followed stood, so it takes the past simple.
  67. We waited for the thunder after the flash of lightning, but we ___ (not hear) anything.
    Accepted answers: didn't hear / did not hear
    Hear is not normally used in the continuous, so the negative past simple didn't + infinitive is used.
  68. When I came downstairs, the TV was still on and the dog ___ (snore) on the couch.
    Answer: was snoring
    The snoring was already in progress when I came down, so the past continuous describes the scene.
  69. When we walked down to the pond after dinner, the frogs ___ (make) a terrible racket.
    Answer: were making
    The noise was in progress when we got there, so the past continuous sets the scene.
  70. Water ___ (drip) from the ceiling when we got back from vacation, so we called a plumber.
    Answer: was dripping
    The dripping had started before we got back and was still going on, so the past continuous is used.
  71. It was so hot in the classroom that Mr. Diaz ___ (open) all the windows.
    Answer: opened
    Opening the windows is a completed action, so it takes the past simple.
  72. Before crossing the road, the little girl ___ (look) both ways twice.
    Answer: looked
    Looking both ways is a completed action, so it takes the past simple.
  73. By the time we arrived, the parking garage ___ (be) completely full.
    Answer: was
    Be is a stative verb, so the state of the garage at that time takes the past simple.
  74. When we reached the top of the hill, the sun ___ (shine) and we could see for miles.
    Answer: was shining
    The past continuous describes the background: the sun was shining when we arrived.
  75. For a moment, Ivan ___ (think) he recognized the woman on the train, but she was a stranger.
    Answer: thought
    Think meaning believe is a stative verb, so it takes the past simple.
  76. Did you see that? I'm sure I ___ (see) a fox run across the road just now.
    Answer: saw
    Just now refers to a finished moment, so the past simple is used; see is not normally used in the continuous.
  77. We couldn't see the waterfall through the trees, but we ___ (can certainly hear) it.
    Answer: could certainly hear
    The past of can is could, and the adverb certainly goes between could and hear.
  78. Halfway across the bridge, Paulo ___ (see) a small boat drifting toward the rocks.
    Answer: saw
    Seeing the boat is a completed event at one moment, so it takes the past simple.
  79. The package on the doorstep ___ (be) much heavier than I expected.
    Answer: was
    Be is a stative verb, so a past state takes the past simple.
  80. When I last saw my brother at the station, he ___ (wear) a green jacket and a baseball cap.
    Answer: was wearing
    The past continuous describes what someone had on at a particular moment in the past.
  81. When we finally found the cat, she ___ (hide) under the guest bed.
    Answer: was hiding
    The hiding was in progress when we found her, so the past continuous is used.
  82. When it started to pour, everyone in the park ___ (run) for cover under the trees.
    Answer: ran
    Running for cover was a completed reaction after the rain started, so it takes the past simple.
