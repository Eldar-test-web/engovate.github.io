Essential Grammar in Use Unit 24 – Regular and Irregular Verbs

Choose or type the answer to each question, then press Check to see if you are right.
  1. My little brother ___ (make) a birthday card for Mom yesterday.
    Answer: made
    'Make' is irregular. Its past simple and past participle are both 'made'.
  2. I ___ (cut) my finger while I was making dinner.
    Answer: cut
    'Cut' is irregular and does not change: the past simple and past participle are both 'cut'.
  3. I ___ (get) a text message from Priya a few minutes ago.
    Answer: got
    'A few minutes ago' needs the past simple. 'Get' is irregular: the past simple is 'got'.
  4. Thanks for the flowers! Nobody has ever ___ (bring) me flowers before.
    Answer: brought
    After 'has' use the past participle. 'Bring' is irregular: the past simple and past participle are both 'brought'.
  5. We ___ (pay) $40 for our concert tickets last month.
    Answer: paid
    'Pay' is irregular in spelling: the past simple and past participle are 'paid', not 'payed'.
  6. Have you ___ (enjoy) your stay at our hotel?
    Answer: enjoyed
    After 'have' use the past participle. 'Enjoy' is regular, so add -ed: 'enjoyed'.
  7. Ella ___ (buy) a new pair of running shoes last Saturday.
    Answer: bought
    'Last Saturday' needs the past simple. 'Buy' is irregular: the past simple and past participle are both 'bought'.
  8. We ___ (sit) in the front row at the concert, so we could see everything.
    Answer: sat
    This is a finished past event, so use the past simple. 'Sit' is irregular: the past form is 'sat'.
  9. The train has already ___ (leave), so we'll have to wait for the next one.
    Answer: left
    After 'has' use the past participle. 'Leave' is irregular: the past simple and past participle are both 'left'.
  10. Something funny ___ (happen) at school yesterday.
    Answer: happened
    'Yesterday' needs the past simple. 'Happen' is regular, so add -ed: 'happened'.
  11. Have you ___ (hear) the news? Ryan is getting married!
    Answer: heard
    After 'have' use the past participle. 'Hear' is irregular: the past simple and past participle are both 'heard'.
  12. I ___ (put) my keys on the table when I came in, but now I can't find them.
    Answer: put
    'Put' is irregular and does not change: the past simple and past participle are both 'put'.
  13. My dad ___ (catch) a big fish in the lake last summer.
    Answer: caught
    'Last summer' needs the past simple. 'Catch' is irregular: the past form is 'caught'.
  14. We ___ (watch) a documentary about whales last night.
    Answer: watched
    'Last night' needs the past simple. 'Watch' is regular, so add -ed: 'watched'.
  15. The teacher explained the problem again, and this time everybody ___ (understand) it.
    Answer: understood
    This is a finished past event, so use the past simple. 'Understand' is irregular: the past form is 'understood'.
  16. Oops! I ___ (break) a glass while I was washing the dishes.
    Answer: broke
    This is a finished past event, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'break' is 'broke'.
  17. The classroom window was ___ (break) by a ball during recess.
    Answer: broken
    After 'was' in a passive sentence, use the past participle. The past participle of 'break' is 'broken', not 'broke'.
  18. The movie ___ (begin) at 8:00, but we arrived at 8:15.
    Answer: began
    This is a finished past event, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'begin' is 'began'.
  19. Hurry up! The class has already ___ (begin).
    Answer: begun
    After 'has' use the past participle. The past participle of 'begin' is 'begun', not 'began'.
  20. We ___ (eat) at a great Thai restaurant last night.
    Answer: ate
    'Last night' needs the past simple. The past simple of 'eat' is 'ate'.
  21. Have you ever ___ (eat) sushi?
    Answer: eaten
    After 'have' use the past participle. The past participle of 'eat' is 'eaten', not 'ate'.
  22. After the long run, Nina ___ (drink) two bottles of water.
    Answer: drank
    This is a finished past event, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'drink' is 'drank'.
  23. The baby has ___ (drink) all her milk.
    Answer: drunk
    After 'has' use the past participle. The past participle of 'drink' is 'drunk', not 'drank'.
  24. My aunt ___ (drive) from Chicago to Denver in two days.
    Answer: drove
    This is a finished past trip, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'drive' is 'drove'.
  25. I've never ___ (drive) on the left side of the road.
    Answer: driven
    After 'have' ('ve) use the past participle. The past participle of 'drive' is 'driven', not 'drove'.
  26. The president ___ (speak) on TV last night.
    Answer: spoke
    'Last night' needs the past simple. The past simple of 'speak' is 'spoke'.
  27. Have you ___ (speak) to the new manager yet?
    Answer: spoken
    After 'have' use the past participle. The past participle of 'speak' is 'spoken', not 'spoke'.
  28. Omar ___ (write) a long email to his teacher last night.
    Answer: wrote
    'Last night' needs the past simple. The past simple of 'write' is 'wrote'.
  29. This song was ___ (write) by two teenagers in their garage.
    Answer: written
    After 'was' in a passive sentence, use the past participle. The past participle of 'write' is 'written', not 'wrote'.
  30. Only five students ___ (come) to class on Friday because of the snow.
    Answer: came
    'On Friday' needs the past simple. The past simple of 'come' is 'came'.
  31. Look who's here! Your cousins have ___ (come) to visit you.
    Answer: come
    After 'have' use the past participle. The past participle of 'come' is 'come', the same as the base form.
  32. I ___ (know) the answer, but I was too shy to say it.
    Answer: knew
    This is in the past, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'know' is 'knew'.
  33. This painter wasn't well ___ (know) until after he died.
    Answer: known
    After 'wasn't' in a passive sentence, use the past participle. The past participle of 'know' is 'known', not 'knew'.
  34. When we arrived, we ___ (take) a taxi from the airport to the hotel.
    Answer: took
    This is a finished past trip, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'take' is 'took'.
  35. Who has ___ (take) my pen? It was on my desk a minute ago.
    Answer: taken
    After 'has' use the past participle. The past participle of 'take' is 'taken', not 'took'.
  36. Last weekend we ___ (go) hiking in the mountains.
    Answer: went
    'Last weekend' needs the past simple. The past simple of 'go' is 'went'.
  37. Ethan isn't here right now. He's ___ (go) to the bank.
    Answer: gone
    'He's' here means 'he has', so use the past participle. The past participle of 'go' is 'gone', not 'went'.
  38. Last year my teacher ___ (give) me a good book to read over the summer.
    Answer: gave
    This is a finished past event, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'give' is 'gave'.
  39. Have you ___ (give) Hannah her birthday present yet?
    Answer: given
    After 'have' use the past participle. The past participle of 'give' is 'given', not 'gave'.
  40. The boy ___ (throw) the ball over the fence and then ran to get it.
    Answer: threw
    This is a finished past event, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'throw' is 'threw'.
  41. A lot of food is ___ (throw) away every day.
    Answer: thrown
    After 'is' in a passive sentence, use the past participle. The past participle of 'throw' is 'thrown', not 'threw'.
  42. I ___ (forget) my umbrella, so I got wet on the way home.
    Answer: forgot
    This is a finished past event, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'forget' is 'forgot'.
  43. Oh no, I've ___ (forget) my password again!
    Answer: forgotten
    After 'have' ('ve) use the past participle. The past participle of 'forget' is 'forgotten', not 'forgot'.
  44. Leo ___ (wash) the car last Sunday afternoon.
    Answer: washed
    'Last Sunday afternoon' needs the past simple. 'Wash' is regular, so add -ed: 'washed'.
  45. Be careful! Sofia has ___ (break) a cup, and there are small pieces on the floor.
    Answer: broken
    After 'has' use the past participle. The past participle of 'break' is 'broken'.
  46. The kids were so tired that they ___ (sleep) for ten hours.
    Answer: slept
    This is in the past, so use the past simple. 'Sleep' is irregular: the past form is 'slept'.
  47. I ___ (see) a famous actor at the airport yesterday morning.
    Answer: saw
    This is a finished past event, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'see' is 'saw', not 'seen'.
  48. It ___ (rain) three times last week, so the garden looks very green now.
    Answer: rained
    'Last week' is a finished time, so use the past simple. 'Rain' is regular, so add -ed: 'rained'.
  49. Max can't get into his apartment because he's ___ (lose) his keys.
    Answer: lost
    'He's' here means 'he has', so use the past participle. The past participle of 'lose' is 'lost'.
  50. Have you ever ___ (see) a whale?
    Answer: seen
    After 'have' use the past participle. The past participle of 'see' is 'seen', not 'saw'.
  51. Over 300 cars were ___ (steal) in the city last year.
    Answer: stolen
    After 'were' in a passive sentence, use the past participle. The past participle of 'steal' is 'stolen'.
  52. Two years ago, my sister ___ (go) to Canada to study.
    Answer: went
    'Two years ago' needs the past simple. The past simple of 'go' is 'went'.
  53. I've ___ (finish) my homework, so can I watch TV now?
    Answer: finished
    After 'have' ('ve) use the past participle. 'Finish' is regular, so add -ed: 'finished'.
  54. The first skyscrapers were ___ (build) in Chicago in the 1880s.
    Answer: built
    After 'were' in a passive sentence, use the past participle. 'Build' is irregular: the past participle is 'built'.
  55. Kenji ___ (learn) a lot of Spanish when he lived in Mexico.
    Accepted answers: learned / learnt
    'When he lived in Mexico' is past time, so use the past simple. In American English the past form of 'learn' is 'learned'.
  56. This is the first time I've ___ (ride) on a roller coaster.
    Answer: ridden
    After 'have' ('ve) use the past participle. The past participle of 'ride' is 'ridden', not 'rode'.
  57. How long have you ___ (know) your best friend?
    Answer: known
    After 'have' use the past participle. The past participle of 'know' is 'known', not 'knew'.
  58. The little boy ___ (fall) off his bike on the way to school.
    Answer: fell
    This is a finished past event, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'fall' is 'fell'.
  59. Grace ___ (hurt) her back when she lifted a heavy box last week.
    Answer: hurt
    'Last week' needs the past simple. 'Hurt' is irregular and does not change: the past form is 'hurt'.
  60. When the door opened, our dog ___ (run) out into the street.
    Answer: ran
    This is a finished past event, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'run' is 'ran'.
  61. I've never ___ (run) more than five kilometers.
    Answer: run
    After 'have' ('ve) use the past participle. The past participle of 'run' is 'run', the same as the base form.
  62. Look at this! The kids have ___ (make) a snowman in the yard.
    Answer: made
    After 'have' use the past participle. The past participle of 'make' is 'made'.
  63. I haven't ___ (tell) anyone about the surprise party.
    Answer: told
    After 'haven't' use the past participle. The past participle of 'tell' is 'told'.
  64. Hannah ___ (win) first prize in the school art competition last month.
    Answer: won
    'Last month' needs the past simple. The past simple of 'win' is 'won'.
  65. We've ___ (meet) a lot of friendly people on this trip.
    Answer: met
    After 'have' ('ve) use the past participle. The past participle of 'meet' is 'met'.
  66. I was ___ (wake up) at 3 a.m. by a phone call. It was a wrong number!
    Answer: woken up
    After 'was' in a passive sentence, use the past participle. The past participle of 'wake up' is 'woken up', not 'woke up'.
  67. Last Saturday, I ___ (swim) 40 laps at the sports center.
    Answer: swam
    'Last Saturday' needs the past simple. The past simple of 'swim' is 'swam', not 'swum'.
  68. At first I ___ (think) the movie was boring, but the ending was great.
    Answer: thought
    'At first' shows this was in the past, so use the past simple. The past simple of 'think' is 'thought'.
  69. English is ___ (speak) at all the meetings in our company.
    Answer: spoken
    After 'is' in a passive sentence, use the past participle. The past participle of 'speak' is 'spoken'.
  70. My new phone ___ (cost) $800 when I bought it last year.
    Answer: cost
    'Last year' needs the past simple. 'Cost' is irregular and does not change: the past form is 'cost'.
  71. My uncle has ___ (drive) a taxi in New York for twenty years.
    Answer: driven
    After 'has' use the past participle. The past participle of 'drive' is 'driven'.
  72. Those cookies were delicious. They were all ___ (sell) by 10 a.m.
    Answer: sold
    After 'were' in a passive sentence, use the past participle. The past participle of 'sell' is 'sold'.
  73. We ___ (fly) from Miami to Lima last week.
    Answer: flew
    'Last week' needs the past simple. The past simple of 'fly' is 'flew'.
