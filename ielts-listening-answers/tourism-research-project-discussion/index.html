Tourism Research Project Discussion - IELTS Listening Answers & Explanations

From IELTS Vol 2 Academic Listening Test 10 · Part 3 · Questions 21–30

Questions

Questions 21–26 One Choice

Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.

Tourism research

21 Mike suggests they use the word area to mean
  1. geographical location.
  2. tourist site.
  3. research field.
22 According to Eva, most people believe that culture
  1. refers to long-established events and places.
  2. includes modern forms of art and entertainment.
  3. is becoming less linked to particular regions.
23 Why should they carry out about 200 surveys?
  1. to provide information about a range of events
  2. to be able to meet the deadline
  3. to get valid results
24 Which groups of people do they decide to interview?
  1. domestic tourists and local residents
  2. local residents and international tourists
  3. international and domestic tourists
25 Mike expresses concern at having to ask about
  1. the amount of money a person has spent
  2. a person's age
  3. a person's occupation
26 Which form will their results be presented in?
  1. a table
  2. a pie chart
  3. a graph

Questions 27–30 Matching

Which tourist attraction will be highlighted in each of the following countries?

Choose FOUR answers from the box and write the correct letter, A-G.

A. ancient buildings

B. contemporary art gallery

C. food festival

D. museums displaying traditional objects

E. national parks

F. theatre performances

G. traditional dances

27 Mexico
28 Greece
29 Britain
30 India

Answers & Explanations Summary

# Answer Evidence Explanation
Q21 A Oh, it's probably best to use it for place, in the sense of province or whatever Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says Mike thinks the word should mean a place, like a province.
Answer Explanation:
The answer A means 'area' should mean a geographical location.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is A because 'place, in the sense of province' matches 'geographical location'.
Q22 A That would be confirming those old-fashioned ideas that culture has to be something that's at least 100 years old
But that is what most people think		 Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says Mike talks about the idea that culture must be at least 100 years old, and Eva says most people think this.
Answer Explanation:
The answer A means most people believe culture is about long-established events and places.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is A because 'something that's at least 100 years old' matches 'long-established', and Eva says 'that is what most people think'.
Q23 C Hmm, our tutor said we should have at least 200. Otherwise, the information won't actually mean that much Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says the tutor wants at least 200 surveys, or the information will not mean much.
Answer Explanation:
The answer C means they need about 200 surveys to get valid results.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is C because 'the information won't actually mean that much' matches 'valid results'. The deadline is mentioned for a different point.
Q24 C In fact, the more I think about it, the more I think we should just go for the tourists, but from overseas as well as national Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says Mike thinks they should ask only tourists, both from other countries and from their own.
Answer Explanation:
The answer C means they will interview international and domestic tourists.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is C because 'from overseas as well as national' matches 'international and domestic tourists'. Local people are left out.
Q25 B But the idea of asking people how old they are Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says Mike is worried about asking people their age.
Answer Explanation:
The answer B means Mike is worried about asking about a person's age.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is B because 'how old they are' matches 'a person's age'. He lists job and opinion as acceptable background.
Q26 B Pie chart sounds good Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says Eva likes the idea of a pie chart.
Answer Explanation:
The answer B means the results will be shown in a pie chart.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is B because Eva says 'Pie chart sounds good', and Mike also says that most people find pie charts 'visually stimulating'.
Q27 C I found out that Mexico celebrates the fact that it produces a huge number of varieties of chillies, and people flock to this special event to taste dishes using them Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says people go to a special event in Mexico to taste dishes made with chillies.
Answer Explanation:
The answer C means the attraction for Mexico is a food festival.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is C because the 'special event to taste dishes' matches 'food festival'. Museums are mentioned, but they decide on 'a different tack'.
Q28 A And I think we should go for the ruins in this case, the temples and theatres and so on, rather than the food Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says that for Greece they should talk about the ruins, such as temples and theatres.
Answer Explanation:
The answer A means the attraction for Greece is ancient buildings.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is A because 'ruins... temples and theatres' are ancient buildings, and they choose these 'rather than the food'.
Q29 F And for Britain, instead of focusing on the countryside and parks, I'd like to find out more about why people go to see plays and musicals Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says that for Britain, Mike wants to look at why people go to plays and musicals, not at the countryside.
Answer Explanation:
The answer F means the attraction for Britain is theatre performances.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is F because 'plays and musicals' are theatre performances. The countryside and parks are rejected.
Q30 G we could mention all the different folk dances Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says that for India they could talk about the different folk dances.
Answer Explanation:
The answer G means the attraction for India is traditional dances.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is G because 'folk dances' means traditional dances, and they choose them instead of temples or game parks.

Transcript

NARRATOR: Now turn to Section 3. Section 3. You will hear two students called Eva and Mike discussing their research project on tourism. First, you have some time to look at questions 21 to 26. Now listen carefully and answer questions 21 to 26.

Mike: Hi, Eva, is this a good time for us to look at our research project? Our tutor told us to keep the focus narrow. So what about we go for why people choose to go to certain places and attractions, and leave the rest out?

Eva: You're right. Otherwise, we'll have far too much information to cope with. Okay, so what about the survey?

Mike: Well, I've looked through the ideas we had for questions for it, and I think it's not clear how we use the word "area". Sometimes it seems to mean a place, other times the field of our research, and even tourist attraction in some cases.

Eva: Oh, I think that's my fault. I typed out our notes.

Mike: Oh, it's probably best to use it for place, in the sense of province or whatever.

Eva: Yes, I think so. You know, we decided to concentrate on culture and tourism. I wonder if we're really saying culture in the same way as most people do. Because on our list of places to carry out our surveys, I see you've put the Round Music Festival, which is contemporary music. I thought we were going to be looking at more traditional forms of culture.

Mike: That would be confirming those old-fashioned ideas that culture has to be something that's at least 100 years old.

Eva: But that is what most people think.

Mike: Well, let's not get into an argument about that right now. Let's think about the number of completed questionnaires we need. In total, how many questionnaires do you think we have to get? 100? 200? And over how many different events?

Eva: Hmm, our tutor said we should have at least 200. Otherwise, the information won't actually mean that much. And I think it's not practical to try to go to more than about five events. We've got to get the first draft in by the end of February.

Mike: And do you think we should interview everybody? Or just tourists?

Eva: Hmm, because presumably there'd be local people, domestic tourists and international tourists at some events.

Mike: In fact, the more I think about it, the more I think we should just go for the tourists, but from overseas as well as national. I'm sure we'd be able to provide more interesting results if we did that.

Eva: Right.

Mike: There's one thing that worries me, though.

Eva: What's that?

Mike: We said it's important to get some background on the people who answer our questions, like what's their job, what they thought about the event. But the idea of asking people how old they are.

Eva: Hmm, we can just ask them to tick a box with an age in it and tell them they don't have to do it if they don't want to. We could write in our own estimate when we finish.

Mike: Are you sure that would be okay?

Eva: Yes, it's not the most crucial piece of information they're giving. We're going to have a lot of facts and figures to present. Have you any preference for putting them into a pie chart, a table or a graph?

Mike: Tables are easy to do. But I read that most people find pie charts visually stimulating. I hate reading graphs, because unless they're quite big, you don't get exact figures.

Eva: Pie chart sounds good. How are we going to divide up the work?

Mike: If it's okay with you, I'd like us to meet as often as possible to look at what we've each written up. But we should do the actual writing on our own.

NARRATOR: Before you hear the rest of the discussion, you have some time to look at questions 27 to 30. Now listen and answer questions 27 to 30.

Mike: Okay, would this be a good time to discuss our introduction, an overview of tourism around the world, looking at different tourist attractions in different countries? But there are so many to choose from. So let's narrow it down.

Eva: Absolutely.

Mike: I found out that Mexico celebrates the fact that it produces a huge number of varieties of chillies, and people flock to this special event to taste dishes using them. A lot of people think of Mexico as being the best place to see fantastic museums. That's true. But let's take a different tack. What about Greece? One of the world's most famous countries for tourists.

Eva: Yes. And I think we should go for the ruins in this case, the temples and theatres and so on, rather than the food.

Mike: And for Britain, instead of focusing on the countryside and parks, I'd like to find out more about why people go to see plays and musicals. Is it because there's such a good choice?

Eva: Hmm, interesting point. And for India, instead of concentrating on the temples or the game parks, we could mention all the different folk dances. Every region has its own, and they don't just perform them at festivals. They are part of everyday life.

Mike: Okay, we've got more than enough. Let's draft the introduction.

NARRATOR: That is the end of Section 3. You now have half a minute to check your answers.
