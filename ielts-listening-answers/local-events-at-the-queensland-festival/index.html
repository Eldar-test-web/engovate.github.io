Local Events At The Queensland Festival - IELTS Listening Answers & Explanations

From IELTS Vol 8 Academic Listening Test 8 · Part 2 · Questions 11–20

Questions

Questions 11–16 One Choice

Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.

11 Why does Jane recommend visiting the Music Tent on Tuesday?
  1. There will be free entry.
  2. There will be fewer people.
  3. There will be special classes.
12 When do the educational workshops take place in the Music Tent?
  1. weekends only
  2. every evening
  3. every afternoon
13 Why does Jane recommend going to the library to buy a ticket?
  1. The tickets will be cheaper there.
  2. You can get tickets in advance there.
  3. It's easier to get tickets there than in the city.
14 What will history lovers find interesting about Macquarie House?
  1. its gardens
  2. its architecture
  3. its location
15 The Big Barbecue is different this year because
  1. it will be much larger than before.
  2. there will be free entertainment.
  3. the food will be cooked by international chefs.
16 What can you do at the Railway Museum?
  1. see a steam engine working
  2. dress up in a railway worker's uniform
  3. have a meal inside an old train

Questions 17–18 Two Choices

Choose TWO letters, A-E.

17 18 Which TWO free things does the family ticket on the steam train include?
  1. an educational talk
  2. a children's book
  3. a short film
  4. a flag
  5. a tour of the Ipswich Museum

Questions 19–20 Two Choices

Choose TWO letters, A-E.

19 20 Which TWO things apply when voting for Our Favourite Place?
  1. People of any age may vote.
  2. You may send in your vote by email.
  3. Only local residents may vote.
  4. You may vote several times.
  5. Voting ends at midnight on Saturday.

Answers & Explanations Summary

# Answer Evidence Explanation
Q11 B It's going to be lots of fun, but if you're free, it's probably a better option to leave it till Tuesday, unless you love crowds Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says Monday will be fun but busy, so it is better to go on Tuesday unless you love crowds.
Answer Explanation:
The answer B means Jane recommends Tuesday because there will be fewer people.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is B because 'unless you love crowds' shows that Tuesday has fewer people. Masterclasses on Tuesday need registration, so they are not free entry.
Q12 C They now run each afternoon from one till four o'clock before the evening performances start from five Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says the workshops now run every afternoon from one to four o'clock, before the evening shows.
Answer Explanation:
The answer C means the educational workshops take place every afternoon.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is C because 'each afternoon' matches 'every afternoon'. They were weekend workshops, but they have been extended.
Q13 A As a local resident, you will be able to purchase discounted tickets Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says local residents can buy discounted tickets at a library.
Answer Explanation:
The answer A means the tickets are cheaper at the library.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is A because 'discounted' means cheaper, and 'for the best deal' shows the library price is best.
Q14 C This building has a fascinating history because it was built on the very same spot where the first settlers landed Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says the house has a fascinating history because it stands on the same spot where the first settlers landed.
Answer Explanation:
The answer C means history lovers will find the location of Macquarie House interesting.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is C because 'the very same spot' means its location. The gardens are only for walking or picnics.
Q15 B there'll be local and international bands playing on the main stage for the first time this year at no extra cost Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says there will be bands on the main stage for the first time this year, at no extra cost.
Answer Explanation:
The answer B means the Big Barbecue is different this year because there is free entertainment.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is B because 'no extra cost' means free, and 'bands playing' is entertainment. It is 'not quite as big' as before, and local chefs cook the food.
Q16 C You won't be able to see it in operation, but you can hop on board and have a meal in the old dining carriage Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says you cannot see the old steam train working, but you can get on and eat a meal in the old dining carriage.
Answer Explanation:
The answer C means visitors can have a meal inside an old train.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is C because 'old dining carriage' means an old train, and 'have a meal' is the same in the option.
Q17 — —
Q18 B / D On the return train journey, the kids get a lovely activity book designed to teach them about the history of the steam train
If you purchase a family ticket, as well as a trip on the train, you receive a free Australian flag each to wave while aboard		 Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says that on the way back the children get an activity book about the history of the train. It also says family ticket buyers get a free Australian flag each.
Answer Explanation:
The answer is B and D. This means the family ticket includes a children's book and a flag.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is B because an 'activity book' for 'the kids' is a children's book. D is correct because 'free Australian flag' matches 'a flag'. The museum tour and film cost extra.
Q19 — —
Q20 A / D Also, remember that no age limit applies for this one
You can vote as often as you like by sending in the voting form		 Excerpt/Transcript Explanation:
The transcript says there is no age limit for this event. It also says you can vote as often as you like.
Answer Explanation:
The answer is A and D. This means people of any age may vote, and you may vote several times.
Reason For Correctness:
The correct answer is A because 'no age limit applies' means any age may vote. D is correct because 'as often as you like' means several times.

Transcript

Part two. You will hear a tourism representative recommending some local events on a radio programme. First, you have some time to look at questions 11 to 16. Now listen carefully and answer questions 11 to 16.

Good afternoon, listeners. Thanks for tuning in to 4KB. Jane Lewis is with us from Tourism Queensland to tell us about the annual Queensland Festival.

Jane, welcome. Morning, John. So much is going on for the next couple of weeks in our festival.

Here in the state's capital of Brisbane, the music tent opens this week, and this will be an event not to be missed. As the name suggests, the music tent is an enormous tent where musical performances and activities will be going on throughout the day for the next two weeks. Monday is going to be really busy as it's a public holiday and the first day of the event.

It's going to be lots of fun, but if you're free, it's probably a better option to leave it till Tuesday, unless you love crowds. The following day is when the masterclasses take place, and this requires pre-registration, so you can't just turn up, I'm afraid. The weekend educational workshops were so popular last year that they have been extended this year.

They now run each afternoon from one till four o'clock before the evening performances start from five. If you're learning to play an instrument, these workshops are great fun, so make sure you get along there. Tickets for the music tent events are available from the door or you can purchase them at the ticket office located in the city centre.

For the best deal, though, take proof of your home address to any library. As a local resident, you will be able to purchase discounted tickets. Apart from music, we'll also be celebrating our local history with a special walking tour around Brisbane's city centre.

Not to be missed on the walk is a tour of Macquarie House, the home of Queensland's first governor. It has lovely gardens you can walk around or picnic in. This building has a fascinating history because it was built on the very same spot where the first settlers landed, so it's really worth a visit if you enjoy learning about our local history.

No Australian event is complete without a barbie, and I think the Big Barbecue is going to be fantastic. It's not quite as big as the Millennium Year Barbecue, but there'll be local and international bands playing on the main stage for the first time this year at no extra cost. As always, the food will be prepared by top local chefs.

My mouth is watering already. I really recommend going along to the Railway Museum during the festival because trains are such an important part of Queensland's history. On loan from Queensland Rail at the moment is our very first steam train.

You won't be able to see it in operation, but you can hop on board and have a meal in the old dining carriage. There is also a wonderful display of uniforms that have been worn over the last 100 years. Before you hear the rest of the talk, you have some time to look at questions 17 to 20.

Now listen and answer questions 17 to 20. Another really special opportunity is a return trip on our only remaining steam train, which runs from Ipswich to Toowoomba. If you purchase a family ticket, as well as a trip on the train, you receive a free Australian flag each to wave while aboard.

There is a small charge for the Ipswich museum tour, but I think it's worth it as it makes an educational day out. The documentary film you can see there is interesting and it's well worth the $2 entry fee. On the return train journey, the kids get a lovely activity book designed to teach them about the history of the steam train.

Don't forget to bring your sunscreen and hats. It's going to be hot up there in Toowoomba at this time of year. And finally, I'm going to tell you about an event called Our Favourite Place.

This is an opportunity for the public to vote for their favourite spot anywhere in the state. Maybe a park or restaurant. It's up to you.

You can vote even if you're not living in the area at the moment. You can vote as often as you like by sending in the voting form or if you prefer, send a text message from any Australian mobile phone number with the name of the location. It's so easy.

Not much time left, though, so get those votes in as soon as possible. The winning location will be announced on the last Saturday of the festival. Also, remember that no age limit applies for this one.

So this is a great way to get the whole family involved. Well, thank you very much for that, Jane. Now, we have... That is the end of part two.

You now have 30 seconds to check your answers to part two.
