Band-9 Samples: Some children spend hours every day on their smartphones. Why is this the case? Do you think this is a positive or a negative development?

April 11, 2023 / Band 9, Cambridge IELTS 17 Academic, IELTS Writing Task 2

This post contains ideas and band-9 sample essays for the Cambridge IELTS 17 Academic Writing Task 2 Test 2 topic:

“Some children spend hours every day on their smartphones. Why is this the case? Do you think this is a positive or a negative development?”

Brainstorming Phases

It’s advisable for you to attempt writing your own essay utilizing these ideas, examples, synonyms, advanced vocabulary, and proposed structures prior to examining any band-9 model essays.

Positive & Negative Ideas

Positives:

  1. Enhancing communication skills through texting and social apps.
  2. Fostering critical thinking through online discussions and debates.
  3. Acquiring new languages using language-learning apps.
  4. Developing problem-solving abilities via educational games.
  5. Staying up-to-date with current events and news.
  6. Strengthening friendships by connecting with peers.
  7. Gaining exposure to diverse cultures and global perspectives.
  8. Building digital literacy and technical competence.
  9. Cultivating empathy through exposure to diverse viewpoints.
  10. Discovering new interests and hobbies online.

Negatives:

  1. Hindering face-to-face social interaction skills.
  2. Distracting from outdoor activities and nature appreciation.
  3. Eroding attention spans and focus capabilities.
  4. Increasing exposure to cyberbullying and online harassment.
  5. Exposing children to inappropriate content and information.
  6. Impairing academic performance and study habits.
  7. Promoting addiction to social media and gaming.
  8. Encouraging procrastination and difficulty with time management.
  9. Leading to privacy breaches and personal data risks.
  10. Causing physical and mental health issues.

Real-Life Examples To Make Ideas Clearer

Favoring positives:

  1. Smartphone apps, such as Duolingo and Quizlet, have helped children in developing countries to learn new languages more efficiently, enabling them to access better educational and job opportunities in the future.
  2. Children using educational apps like Khan Academy Kids gain exposure to a wide range of subjects, building digital literacy and technical competence while reinforcing foundational knowledge in areas such as math, language arts, and science.
  3. Engaging with puzzle and strategy games on smartphones, such as Monument Valley and Plants vs. Zombies, allows children to exercise their problem-solving abilities, enhance critical thinking skills, and foster creativity in overcoming challenges.
  4. During the COVID-19 pandemic, smartphones played a crucial role in facilitating remote learning for children, allowing them to attend virtual classes and collaborate with classmates on group projects, ensuring the continuity of their education despite the challenging circumstances.
  5. In the early stages of the COVID-19 pandemic, children with smartphones could quickly access safety information, updates, and communicate with family members, illustrating the life-saving value of these devices in emergencies.
  6. In rural areas with limited resources, smartphones have empowered children to access quality educational content, enabling them to improve their knowledge and skills despite the lack of traditional educational infrastructure.
  7. Children with disabilities have greatly benefited from smartphones as they provide access to specialized apps and support platforms that cater to their unique needs, promoting inclusivity and equal opportunities in education.
  8. By joining interest-based groups on social media platforms such as Facebook, children can discover like-minded peers and participate in meaningful discussions about their favorite topics, fostering a sense of belonging and enabling them to develop strong and supportive friendships.
  9. Through social media platforms like Twitter, young people from different countries engage in discussions about global issues and cultural topics, resulting in a greater understanding and appreciation of the diverse perspectives and values that exist around the world.
  10. Using smartphones for creative pursuits, such as photography or digital art, can help children develop their artistic skills and discover new hobbies or potential career paths.

Favoring negatives:

  1. Exposure to adult websites on smartphones can lead to the premature introduction of explicit content to children, potentially distorting their understanding of healthy relationships and leading to the development of unrealistic expectations regarding intimacy and consent.
  2. The fast-paced nature of TikTok can contribute to the development of a shorter attention span in children, as they become accustomed to rapid information intake and may struggle to remain focused on longer, more in-depth tasks or materials.
  3. Prolonged smartphone use can contribute to an increased risk of developing musculoskeletal issues in children, such as “text neck” or “texting thumb,” which result from maintaining awkward postures and repetitive strain while using their devices.
  4. Excessive exposure to smartphone screens can result in vision problems, like digital eye strain, as children continually focus on small, bright displays.
  5. Overreliance on smartphones may hinder the development of essential life skills, such as problem-solving, navigation, and time management, as children come to depend on technology for every task.
  6. The prevalence of cyberbullying on social media platforms can have severe consequences for children, as hurtful comments, exclusion, and harassment can lead to emotional distress, lowered self-esteem, and even long-term mental health issues, such as anxiety and depression.
  7. Excessive smartphone use can contribute to the development of addictive behaviors, as children may become reliant on the dopamine rush from social media interactions, gaming, and other online activities.
  8. Smartphone overuse can negatively impact creativity, as children may spend less time engaging in imaginative play or pursuing hobbies, instead relying on curated content and passive consumption for entertainment.
  9. Children may become more comfortable with digital interactions and struggle to effectively express themselves, read facial cues, or build meaningful connections in person.
  10. Frequent use of smartphones can make children more susceptible to oversharing personal information on social media and messaging apps, potentially leading to unintended consequences, such as cyberstalking or real-life safety concerns.

Advanced Vocabulary

  1. Pervasive: widespread, present, or noticeable in every aspect of a particular thing or place.
    • Example: The pervasive nature of smartphones in modern society has led to an increasing number of children spending hours each day on these devices.
  2. Ubiquitous: present, appearing, or found everywhere.
    • Example: With the ubiquitous presence of smartphones, children are more exposed to digital technology, which can lead to increased screen time.
  3. Sedentary: characterized by much sitting and little physical exercise.
    • Example: The sedentary lifestyle associated with prolonged smartphone use can contribute to obesity and other health issues in children.
  4. Impede: delay or prevent something by obstructing it.
    • Example: Excessive smartphone use can impede children’s academic performance and hinder their overall development.
  5. Exacerbate: make a problem, bad situation, or negative feeling worse.
    • Example: Spending hours on smartphones might exacerbate children’s existing behavioral and emotional issues.
  6. Adept: very skilled or proficient at something.
    • Example: Children have become adept at using smartphones, which can lead to increased usage and dependence on these devices.
  7. Conducive: making a certain situation or outcome likely or possible.
    • Example: The captivating nature of smartphone apps and games is conducive to children spending hours on their devices.
  8. Compromise: weaken or harm something.
    • Example: Excessive smartphone use can compromise children’s social skills, as they may prioritize screen time over face-to-face interactions.
  9. Supplant: supersede and replace.
    • Example: In some cases, smartphones have supplanted traditional methods of learning and communication for children.
  10. Vigilant: keeping careful watch for possible danger or difficulties.
    • Example: Parents should remain vigilant about their children’s smartphone usage, monitoring the time spent on devices and ensuring a healthy balance of activities.

Advanced Phrases & Collocations

  1. Inextricably intertwined: Describing two or more things that are closely connected and cannot be easily separated or distinguished from each other.
    • Example: In today’s digital age, the lives of children and adolescents are inextricably intertwined with technology, which explains why they often spend hours on their smartphones daily.
  2. Digital literacy: The skills and knowledge required to effectively use and navigate digital technologies and the internet.
    • Example: While spending hours on smartphones can be seen as a negative development, it is worth noting that this exposure may also foster digital literacy, which is increasingly important in today’s technology-driven world.
  3. Information overload: The overwhelming amount of information and stimuli that individuals are exposed to, particularly through digital devices and the internet.
    • Example: Although access to smartphones can provide children with a wealth of knowledge and resources, this constant barrage of information may also lead to information overload, which can adversely affect their ability to focus and process new information effectively.
  4. Technological proficiency: An individual’s ability and skill in using, understanding, and interacting with various technological devices and platforms.
    • Example: While the extensive use of smartphones by children raises concerns, it is important to acknowledge that this familiarity can lead to enhanced technological proficiency, which is increasingly valuable in today’s competitive job market.
  5. Diminished interpersonal skills: The weakening or reduction of an individual’s ability to communicate and interact effectively with others, especially in face-to-face situations.
    • Example: As children become more reliant on their smartphones, there is a growing concern that this dependence may result in diminished interpersonal skills and a decreased ability to form meaningful relationships.
  6. Virtual socialization: The process of interacting and building relationships with others through online platforms and digital communication tools, rather than in-person encounters.
    • Example: While some may argue that the hours children spend on their smartphones can lead to isolation, it is essential to consider that these devices also offer opportunities for virtual socialization, enabling them to connect with peers and maintain long-distance friendships.
  7. Digital native generation: Individuals born and raised during the widespread adoption of digital technology, making them inherently comfortable and familiar with using it.
    • Example: As members of the digital native generation, today’s children are more inclined to spend hours on their smartphones, as these devices have become an integral part of their daily lives and routines.
  8. Multifaceted implications: The various and diverse consequences or effects that may arise from a particular situation or development.
    • Example: The extensive time children spend on their smartphones has multifaceted implications, ranging from enhanced digital literacy to potential negative impacts on their physical and social well-being.
  9. Cognitive development: The growth and maturation of an individual’s mental abilities and processes, such as thinking, learning, and problem-solving.
    • Example: Although the extensive use of smartphones might provide children with access to a wealth of information, it is important to consider the potential impact of this habit on their cognitive development and overall well-being.
  10. Parental supervision: The monitoring and guidance provided by parents or guardians to ensure their children’s safety and well-being, particularly in relation to their use of technology.
    • Example: To mitigate the potential negative effects of children spending excessive time on their smartphones, it is crucial for parents to exercise appropriate parental supervision and establish boundaries for device usage.

Suggested Essay Structures

Below are suggested essay structures of various tones.

Structure 1: Neutral

Introduction

  • Introduce the topic by paraphrasing the statement.
  • Provide a brief overview of the two aspects to be discussed (reasons for children’s smartphone usage and the evaluation of its positive and negative implications).

Body Paragraph 1: Reasons for children’s smartphone usage

  • Introduce the main reason children spend hours on their smartphones daily.
  • Elaborate on the main reason and explain why it has become prevalent.
  • Present a secondary reason related to the main reason.
  • Explain the secondary reason and its contribution to the overall factors driving children’s smartphone usage.

Body Paragraph 2: Positive implications of children’s smartphone usage

  • Introduce the main advantage of children spending hours on their smartphones daily.
  • Elaborate on the main advantage and explain why it is beneficial.
  • Present a secondary advantage related to the main advantage.
  • Explain the secondary advantage and its contribution to the overall positive implications of children’s smartphone usage.

Body Paragraph 3: Negative implications of children’s smartphone usage

  • Introduce the main disadvantage of children spending hours on their smartphones daily
  • Elaborate on the main disadvantage and explain why it is detrimental.
  • Present a secondary disadvantage related to the main disadvantage.
  • Explain the secondary disadvantage and its contribution to the overall negative implications of children’s smartphone usage.

Conclusion

  • Summarize the reasons and implications of children’s smartphone usage, based on the points discussed in the body paragraphs.
  • Provide a closing statement that emphasizes the importance of understanding and addressing the various aspects of children’s smartphone usage without favoring any side.

Structure 2: Favoring Positives

Introduction

  • Paraphrase the topic statement.
  • Provide a brief overview of the reasons why children spend hours on their smartphones.
  • State that the positive aspects of this development outweigh the negative ones.

Body Paragraph 1: Reasons for children spending hours on smartphones

  • Introduce the primary reason why children spend hours on their smartphones.
  • Elaborate on the primary reason, explaining its significance.
  • Introduce a secondary reason for children spending hours on their smartphones.
  • Elaborate on the secondary reason, explaining its significance.
  • Emphasize the attraction and convenience of smartphones for children.

Body Paragraph 2: Positive aspects of children spending hours on smartphones

  • Introduce the main positive aspect of children spending hours on their smartphones.
  • Elaborate on the main positive aspect, explaining its significance.
  • Introduce a secondary positive aspect of children spending hours on their smartphones.
  • Elaborate on the secondary positive aspect, explaining its significance.
  • Reinforce the overall positive impact of children using smartphones for their personal and educational development.

Body Paragraph 3: Addressing the negative aspects of children spending hours on smartphones

  • Acknowledge the main negative aspect of children spending hours on their smartphones.
  • Explain the main negative aspect, but provide a counterargument to demonstrate why it is not as significant as the positive aspects.
  • Reiterate the importance of children using smartphones effectively and responsibly.

Conclusion

  • Reaffirm that the positive aspects of children spending hours on their smartphones outweigh the negative aspects.
  • Summarize the main points of all body paragraphs.
  • Offer a final thought or call to action, encouraging proper guidance and supervision to maximize the benefits of smartphone usage in children’s lives.

Structure 3: Favoring Negatives

Introduction

  • Paraphrase the topic statement.
  • Provide a brief overview of the reasons why children spend hours on their smartphones.
  • State that the negative aspects of this development outweigh the positive ones.

  • Introduce the primary reason why children spend hours on their smartphones daily.

  • Emphasize the negative implications of these reasons on children’s lives.

Body Paragraph 2: Negative aspects of children spending hours on smartphones

  • Introduce the main negative aspect of children spending hours on their smartphones.
  • Elaborate on the main negative aspect, explaining its significance.
  • Introduce a secondary negative aspect of children spending hours on their smartphones.
  • Elaborate on the secondary negative aspect, explaining its significance.
  • Reinforce the overall negative impact of excessive smartphone usage on children’s well-being and development.

Body Paragraph 3: Addressing the positive aspects of children spending hours on smartphones

  • Acknowledge the main positive aspect of children spending hours on their smartphones.
  • Explain the main positive aspect, but provide a counterargument to demonstrate why it is not as significant as the negative aspects.
  • Reiterate the importance of considering the potential drawbacks and negative consequences of smartphone usage among children.

Conclusion

  • Summarize the main points of all body paragraphs.
  • Offer a final thought or call to action, encouraging parents, educators, and policymakers to implement strategies to mitigate the negative effects of excessive smartphone usage and promote healthier habits for children.

Band-9 Sample Essays

Below are band-9 model essays of various tones and structures.

Sample 1: Neutral

The ubiquity of mobile devices in contemporary society has given rise to a phenomenon where a considerable number of juveniles dedicate numerous hours to utilizing their smartphones daily. This essay aims to explore the underlying causes of this occurrence and ponder whether it constitutes a favorable or unfavorable development, without taking a definitive stance on either perspective.

One primary reason for the extensive use of smartphones by youngsters can be attributed to the exponential expansion of technology and the myriad of applications available at their fingertips. Educational platforms, social media networks, and entertainment options are now easily accessible, catering to the diverse interests and needs of children. For instance, several students rely on online resources to supplement their academic pursuits, thus making portable electronics essential for their learning process.

On the one hand, proponents of this development emphasize the numerous advantages it offers. They argue that cellular phones serve as an indispensable means of communication, enabling children to maintain connections with their peers and family members across vast distances. Furthermore, digital gadgets can be employed to foster creativity and enhance problem-solving skills through the use of educational applications and interactive games. In this sense, the integration of technology into children’s lives can be deemed a positive progression.

On the other hand, critics contend that the detrimental effects of excessive smartphone usage by young people far outweigh the potential benefits. They assert that spending long hours on these devices can lead to a decline in physical activity, resulting in obesity and other health-related issues. Additionally, there is concern about reduced social skills as children become increasingly reliant on virtual interactions, undermining the importance of face-to-face communication.

In conclusion, the widespread use of smartphones among juniors can be attributed to the rapid advancement of technology and the diverse range of applications catering to their interests. While some view this development as a means to enhance communication and learning, others argue that it could have detrimental effects on children’s physical and social well-being. As the discourse surrounding this issue is multifaceted, it is essential to strike a balance between the benefits and drawbacks of smartphone usage to ensure the optimal development of children in today’s digital age.

(365 words)

Sample 2: Neutral

In contemporary society, it is not uncommon to observe minors allocating substantial time to their wireless apparatuses, predominantly smartphones. This essay will delve into the reasons behind this phenomenon and examine the potential merits and drawbacks associated with such behavior, while maintaining a neutral stance.

The pervasiveness of smartphones can be ascribed to their multifaceted nature, which caters to a myriad of interests and provides a seemingly infinite source of entertainment. The younger generation is particularly drawn to social media platforms, where they can join interest-based groups and interact with like-minded individuals. This sense of belonging and camaraderie is inextricably intertwined with their psychological well-being, further exacerbating their reliance on these devices. Moreover, the portability and ease of access to smartphones render it expedient for adolescents to engage with their devices at any given moment, thus augmenting the duration allocated to them.

From one point of view, there are those who argue that this development could potentially be advantageous for the younger populace. For instance, these devices serve as invaluable tools for acquiring knowledge and staying updated about current affairs. Furthermore, they provide a platform for youngsters to express their creativity through various digital mediums, such as photography and videography, as well as develop cognitive skills by participating in interactive games and puzzles.

Conversely, opponents contend that the overuse of these gadgets can have detrimental consequences. One of the most pressing concerns is the exposure to adult websites and explicit content, which might have a deleterious impact on the impressionable minds of children. Additionally, excessive screen time has been linked to a plethora of health issues, including sedentary lifestyles, obesity, and sleep disorders. Not to mention, the constant immersion in the virtual realm may impede the development of essential interpersonal skills, as youngsters become increasingly isolated from their immediate surroundings.

In conclusion, although there are valid arguments on both sides of the debate, it is incontrovertible that the excessive use of compact digital appliances by children is a double-edged sword. While these devices offer numerous opportunities for personal growth and development, the potential risks cannot be overlooked. It is paramount, therefore, for parents and educators to strike a delicate balance in regulating the time and manner in which the younger generation engages with these indispensable technological marvels.

(377 words)

Sample 3: Favoring Positives

The proliferation of pocket-sized electronics has led to a significant shift in the way young individuals interact with technology. In recent years, it has become increasingly common for these youthful users to invest lengthy periods engaging with their communication tools on a daily basis. This essay will examine the reasons behind this phenomenon and argue that, despite some drawbacks, it is largely a positive development.

One reason children devote considerable durations to their smartphones is the accessibility of information. The COVID-19 pandemic has further augmented this trend, as remote learning and social distancing have necessitated the use of digital devices for educational and social purposes. Consequently, pupils have become adept at utilizing their electronic gadgets to facilitate virtual classrooms, collaborating with classmates, and maintaining connections with friends and family, which has proven to be conducive to their overall development.

Upon closer inspection, the positive aspects of this development are manifold. Digital devices have transformed the landscape of education by providing children with access to a wealth of learning materials right at their fingertips. Furthermore, applications like Duolingo and Quizlet have democratized access to knowledge, enabling minors in developing countries to acquire new languages and skills that were once beyond their reach.

Nonetheless, it is crucial to address the potential drawbacks of excessive smartphone usage and present counterarguments to these concerns. One commonly cited issue is the rise of social media platforms like TikTok, which have been criticized for causing a decrease in children’s attention spans. However, it can be argued that this phenomenon encourages juniors to adapt to an increasingly fast-paced digital environment, fostering their ability to process information quickly and efficiently. This skill may prove valuable in the modern world, where rapid decision-making and flexibility are essential for success in various professional and academic contexts.

In conclusion, the positive implications of children’s extensive use of cell phones undoubtedly outweigh the potential disadvantages. The key to ensuring that young individuals maximize their potential while avoiding pitfalls lies in the collaborative efforts of parents, educators, and policymakers. By fostering responsible usage, implementing age-appropriate restrictions, and promoting a balanced approach to online and offline activities, the benefits of smartphones can be harnessed to their fullest potential for the holistic development of children.

(370 words)

Sample 4: Favoring Positives

It is an irrefutable fact that the digital native generation is increasingly immersing themselves in extended periods of smartphone usage. A myriad of factors contribute to this phenomenon, and it has multifaceted implications for the younger demographic. In this essay, I shall delineate the reasons behind this trend and argue that, despite some drawbacks, it is largely a positive development.

Firstly, the shift towards extensive smartphone usage among the youth can largely be attributed to the devices’ multifunctionality and their capacity to cater to a myriad of needs. Smartphones are a veritable treasure trove of information and entertainment, providing access to educational resources, social media platforms, and leisure activities, all within a compact, user-friendly package. Furthermore, children with disabilities have found these gadgets to be invaluable, as they enable them to communicate more effectively and access assistive technologies, significantly improving their quality of life.

Another significant factor that has led to young individuals devoting substantial time to their phones is the ability to engage in global discussions and exchange cultural insights. For instance, Twitter has emerged as a popular forum where adolescents can express their opinions on pressing issues, fostering a sense of global citizenship and cross-cultural understanding. By participating in such conversations, they develop critical thinking skills and a broader worldview, which are essential for their future success.

However, there are concerns that excessive use of smartphones may pose risks to children’s physical well-being. One potential issue is the development of musculoskeletal problems due to prolonged periods of maintaining an unhealthy posture while using the devices. Although this is a legitimate concern, it can be mitigated through awareness campaigns and the implementation of ergonomic solutions, such as adjustable stands and accessories.

In conclusion, the widespread adoption of smartphones by children is a multifaceted phenomenon, driven by the devices’ versatility and their ability to facilitate global engagement. While there are potential negative consequences, such as the risk of musculoskeletal issues, the benefits for children with disabilities and the enrichment of young individuals’ perspectives on global affairs ultimately render this a positive development. It is essential for society to recognize these advantages and to adopt measures to ensure that potential drawbacks are adequately addressed.

(363 words)

Sample 5: Favoring Negatives

In today’s digital era, the pervasive nature of handheld devices has had profound implications on the lives of youngsters. A growing number of children are observed to expend long hours on their smartphones, leading to a myriad of consequences. This essay will explore the reasons behind this phenomenon and argue that it is a predominantly negative development, despite some potential benefits.

The primary reason as to why children allocate substantial spans of time to their smartphones is the vast array of applications available to cater to their multifarious interests. These digital platforms offer instant gratification and an endless supply of entertainment, serving to captivate the attention of impressionable minds. Additionally, as peers become increasingly reliant on digital communication, the fear of missing out on social exchanges compels children to remain constantly connected.

Although increased exposure to technology has been hailed for fostering digital literacy in the younger generation, these benefits are often compromised by the pernicious effects on their physical and emotional well-being. One such concern is the increased incidence of vision problems due to prolonged exposure to screens. For instance, the prevalence of myopia among children has soared in recent years, a phenomenon that has been largely attributed to excessive screen time.

Moreover, the virtual realm has given rise to a myriad of social issues, such as cyberbullying and information overload. The anonymity provided by the internet emboldens nefarious actors to engage in abusive behavior, posing a significant threat to the mental health of vulnerable children. The incessant flow of information, often unfiltered and devoid of context, can also overwhelm young minds, leading to anxiety and confusion.

In conclusion, while there is no denying that smartphones have conferred some benefits upon the younger generation, the adverse consequences associated with excessive usage are of grave concern. It is incumbent upon parents, educators, and policymakers to devise strategies that strike a balance between harnessing the potential of technology and safeguarding the physical and emotional well-being of children.

(326 words)

Sample 6: Favoring Negatives

In today’s fast-paced world, it is not uncommon to observe that a considerable number of adolescents dedicate extended intervals to their mobile devices. This essay will delve into the reasons for this phenomenon and argue that, despite some advantages, this development is predominantly detrimental to young individuals.

There are various factors that contribute to the widespread usage of cell phones among children. Firstly, the inherent appeal of these gadgets lies in their multifunctional nature, offering a plethora of applications and entertainment options such as games, social media, and streaming services. Additionally, the prevalence of virtual socialization has led to an increasing reliance on wireless apparatuses for maintaining friendships and staying connected with peers. In the contemporary era, technological proficiency is seen as a crucial skill, which further encourages teenagers to embrace this digital lifestyle.

Although there are some positive aspects to children using smartphones extensively, such as exploring creative pursuits and discovering potential career paths through online resources, these benefits are overshadowed by a myriad of negative consequences. A chief concern is the vulnerability of young individuals to information breaches and the risk of cyberstalking, which highlights the need for vigilant parental supervision. Moreover, the excessive usage of digital gadgets may also result in physical health issues such as poor posture and vision problems.

Furthermore, one of the paramount drawbacks associated with the ubiquitous employment of mobile devices is the possibility of diminished interpersonal skills. With an increasing tendency to rely on digital communication, adolescents may encounter difficulties in competently communicating, deciphering nonverbal expressions, and forging genuine relationships in person. Consequently, these hindrances can impede their capacity to cultivate enduring bonds and navigate divergent social contexts in the course of their lives.

In conclusion, although smartphones have their merits, the negative implications of children spending countless hours on these devices far outweigh the advantages. It is crucial for parents and educators to recognize the potential risks and promote a balanced approach to technology, ensuring that the younger generation is equipped with the necessary skills to thrive in an increasingly interconnected world.
