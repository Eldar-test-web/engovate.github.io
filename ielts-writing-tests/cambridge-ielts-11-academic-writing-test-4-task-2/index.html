    In conclusion, I firmly believe that economic progress is fundamental to enhance a country in the contemporary global economy. However, it should not be considered an isolated phenomenon, but a set of areas that, if developed, lead to ever-improving results. Band 7

    There is an ongoing debate about whether governments should prioritize economic development above all other issues or if other forms of progress, such as political stability and mass education, should be given preference over financial progress. While many states believe that economic progress should be the most preferred goal due to reducing unemployment problems and maintaining economic stability, there are other groups of individuals who feel that there are other forms of development that need to be highlighted. This essay will explore both sides of the arguments before elucidating my own perspective.

    Proponents of economic progress believe that it should be given priority for several reasons. The first and foremost rationale is that it can reduce unemployment by creating more jobs. When a country witnesses considerable economic development, it has a significant impact on boosting new industries. As a result, more jobs are created, which ultimately lessens the unemployment rate to a large extent. Last but not least, economic progress may help to improve national economic stability. If a country can develop its economy significantly, national output as well as Gross Domestic Product (GDP) will also increase. This generally creates a positive impact on improving the per capita income of its people, which typically helps people’s affordability of consumption.

    However, advocates of other forms of progress think that political stability is one of the major goals that should be given preference above all. In some countries, especially in developing countries, there is more instability due to political chaos, which faces challenges in controlling crime and corruption. Therefore, social unrest is prevalent in those countries, and they generally rank at the bottom in global happiness indexes. Finally, another top priority should be given to national education, as more educated and skilled people can have a positive impact on the economy as a whole.

    In conclusion, though governments have proper grounds to prioritize economic development as a topmost goal, I think there should be a balanced approach by which other issues, such as mass education and maintaining political stability, should also be taken into consideration equally to make a nation progressive. Thus, economic stability and sustainability can be achieved in the long run. (Grammar checked version: band 8)
