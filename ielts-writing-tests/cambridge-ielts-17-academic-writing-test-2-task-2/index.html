    Smartphones have become essential tools for many people worldwide, including the younger generation. Children are often exposed to smartphones by their parents. Some argue that this leads to negative developments, while others claim that they can bring benefits to children. In this essay I will discuss both views while providing my own opinion. From my perspective, I believe that, overall, smartphones have negative developments for children.

    To begin, children develop immense curiosity for many things they encounter in their daily lives. Many parents provide their children with smartphones for entertainment purposes. Some caretakers use their devices to distract children’s attention, allowing them to gain a temporary break and some peace of mind from their children’s constant needs. Furthermore, kids do not have many responsibilities and prefer to spend their time with something that is enjoyable. For example, children enjoy watching their favourite cartoon shows on YouTube. As a result, smartphones become an easy fix for children to spend hours every day on the internet.

    On the one hand, smartphones are designed to make people dependant on their devices. Without proper guidance from adults, mobile phones can bring disadvantages to children. The negative side effects, such as reduced attention spans, excessive dopamine production, and exposure to many negative content, hinder the overall benefits. For example, children can spend hours playing video games and get excessive dopamine hormones, causing them to forget their studies. Consequently, it can impact to their academic performance in school, resulting in lower grades.

    On the other hand, with proper guidance, children can gain valuable knowledge from accessing internet through their mobile devices. It can give them access to information with instant results, allowing them to have a broader perspective and mindset. Moreover, extensive exposure to positive content can motivate them to learn about particular interests that they like. For example, children can learn about fascinating history in an engaging way and enjoy learning subject they once found it boring. As a result, they have more options to learn knowledge that sparks their interest.

    In conclusion, children spend hours in their smartphones because parents give them access. It comes with many challenges, from reduced attention spans to becoming addicted to dopamine. However, with appropriate guidance, it can be a useful tool to excel in their skills. Thus, a balanced approach is needed to avoid children spending hours on the internet. Overall, it brings positive and negative developments, but the negative developments are too significant to ignore.
